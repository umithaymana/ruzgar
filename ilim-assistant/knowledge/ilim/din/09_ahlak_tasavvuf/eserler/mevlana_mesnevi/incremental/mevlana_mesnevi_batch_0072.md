## parça 1432

A certain divine had collected some old rags 
and wound them in his turban,

In order that it might become big and look grand 
when he came into the assembly in the Hatim

He had clipped the rags from garments 
and outwardly embellished the turban with them.

The exterior of the turban was like a robe of Paradise, 
it was shameful and ugly within, like the hypocrite.

Shreds of dervish-cloak (dalg) and cotton and fur

were buried inside that turban.

He had set his face towards the college at dawn, 
that by means of this false dignity he might gain blessings.

A clothes-robber stood waiting on the dark road 
to practice his craft.

He snatched the turban from his head, 
and then started to run in order that he might settle the business.

Thereupon the divine shouted at him, saying, 
“O son, undo the turban, and then take it.
