## parça 2561

He reasoned with him for a long time, and he kept silence, 
suddenly he unveiled the mystery.

Think what of love and passion he whispered into his ear! 
Immediately he made him a crazy wanderer like himself.

He took his hand and accompanied him: 
he too renounced his throne and belt.

These two kings went to distant lands: 
not once has Love committed this crime.

It is honey for the grown-up and milk for children: 
for every boat it is the last bale.

Besides these two, many kings, beyond number, 
have Love torn from their kingdoms and families.

The souls of these three princes also were roaming around China

in every direction, like birds picking up grain.

They dare not open their lips to utter the thoughts hidden, 
because it was a perilous and grave secret.

A hundred thousand heads for a penny

at the moment Love strings his bow in anger.

Even without anger, at the time when he is well-pleased, 
Love is always accustomed to kill recklessly.

This is at the moment when he is contented: 
how shall I describe when he is angered?

But may the soul's pasture be the ransom for his lion 
that is killed by this Love and his scimitar!
