What is this testimony? The making manifest of that which is hidden, 
whether word or act or something else;

For its object is to make manifest the inward nature of the spiritual substance: 
the attributes are permanent, though these accidents are fleeting.

The mark of the gold on the touchstone does not remain, 
the gold remains—of good renown and undoubted.

Similarly, this ritual prayer and holy war and fasting does not remain, 
but the spirit remains in good renown.

The spirit produced certain acts and words of this kind:

As though to say, “My belief is perfect: here is the witness!”, 
but there is doubt as regards the witnesses.

Know that the probity of the witnesses must be established: 
the means of establishing it is a sincerity: you are dependent on that.

In the case of the word-witness, it is keeping your word; 
in the case of the act-witness, it is keeping your covenant.

The word-witness is rejected if it speaks falsely, 
and the act-witness is rejected if it does not run straight.

## parça 1717

He catches his prey ignorantly, like a trap: 
what knowledge has the trap concerning the purpose of its action?

What harm to the trap, or what benefit, from catching? 
I wonder at its idle catching.

O brother, you have uplifted your friends

with two hundred marks of affection, and abandoned.

This has been your business from the hour of birth: 
to catch people with the trap of love.

From that pursuit and throng and vainglory and self existence 
will you get any warp or woof? Try and see!

Most is gone and the day is late; 
you are still busy in pursuit of people.

Go on catching one and releasing another from the trap 
and pursuing another, like mean folk;

Then again release this one and seek the other! 
Here’s a game of heedless children!

Night comes, and nothing is caught in your trap: 
the trap is naught but a headache and shackle to you.

Therefore you were catching yourself with the trap, 
for you are imprisoned and disappointed of your desire.

Is any owner of a trap in the world such a dolt 
that, like us, he tries to catch himself?
