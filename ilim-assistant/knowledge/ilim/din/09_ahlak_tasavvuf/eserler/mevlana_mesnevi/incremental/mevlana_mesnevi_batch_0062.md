“Go not in that direction! Bethink you, O misguided one; 
for you will become captive to distress and poverty.

You will become destitute, you will be cut off from friends, 
you will be despised, and you will feel sorry.”

you flee away from certain truth into error,

Saying, “Ho, to-morrow is mine and after to-morrow:

so that the cry is raised.

for a while, you make yourself a man;

saying, “I will not shrink from any danger.”

“Be afraid and turn away from the sword of poverty!”

and cast off that armour of knowledge and virtue.

you have laid down the blanket in such darkness as this!

and taken hold of their throats,

as the spirits of the infidels who dwell in the tombs.
