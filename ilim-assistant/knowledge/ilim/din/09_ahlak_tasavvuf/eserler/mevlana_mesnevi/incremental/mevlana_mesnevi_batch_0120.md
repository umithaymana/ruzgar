## parça 2390

is stinking and full of scorpions and snakes.

and the rose-tress and the watering-place;

made of the “protecting” mountain a ship of safety.

become as the ghoul or brigand to the wayfarer!

so that they escape from the mischief of philosophy.

in order that mercy may descend on you at every moment.
