## parça 2593

We have said this many a time, O Hasan: 
I will never be weary of setting it forth.

Many a time have you eaten bread to prevent getting thin: 
it is the same bread: why are not you surfeited?

That weariness, then, is caused by lack of hunger and complete indigestion,

not by repetition of the discourse.

How is it that you are not weary of your shop

How is it that you have not been surfeited by speaking ill of men

Passion makes the old medicine new; 
passion lops every bough of weariness.

seek passion, seek passion, passion, passion!

if it seems cold and delicious at the moment of drinking,

the sweet water by which a hundred plants are made to grow.
