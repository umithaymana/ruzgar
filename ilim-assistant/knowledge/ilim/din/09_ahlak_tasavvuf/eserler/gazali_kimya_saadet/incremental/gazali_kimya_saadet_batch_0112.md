Jealousy is defined as your dislike over the 
grant of favour to someone else and your desire that 
the said favour be snatched away from him.

According to the prophetic tradition it is 
unlawful. Another proof of its being unlawful is that it 
proves disliking of Divine command and inner 
impurity. The reason is that the desire of snatching 
away of a favour granted to a man is nothing but 
impurity of the inner self. But if you desire that you 
should also be granted with such favour and you do
