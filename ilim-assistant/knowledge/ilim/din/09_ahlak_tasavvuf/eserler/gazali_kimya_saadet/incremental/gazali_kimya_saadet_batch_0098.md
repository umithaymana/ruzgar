If a man decides to go into solitude, he should 
do so principally with the ('Niyyat') intention of 
safeguarding himself from the 'Shar' (Mischief) of the

the world. Carnal desires and the cursed calumnies of 
the people will not beset him. The more forgetful he 
becomes of what people think or say about him, the 
better it is for him. If his aim is his 'Akhirat' and his
