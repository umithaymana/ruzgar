The Jumua prayers are not essential or 'Farz' for 
women, children and the slaves. Those who are invalid, 
ill or unable to go for 'Jumua' prayers due to 
insurmountable deterrents in the way can say 'Zuhr' (the

prayer should be offered after the Jumua prayers have 
concluded.
