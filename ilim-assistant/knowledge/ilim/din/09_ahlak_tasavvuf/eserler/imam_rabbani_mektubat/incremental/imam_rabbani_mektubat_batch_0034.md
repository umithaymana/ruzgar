Question: Someone has said, All the knowledge is 
contained within two or three letters. Is it credible?

Answer: That person may have known it, heard 
it, or learned it from a book, for it has come from 
the masters of the later era. Hazrat ‘Ali (may Al- 
lah brighten his face, karama ‘ahu ta‘ala wajhahu) 
said, All the knowledge is in the letter ba of bismi 
‘lah, instead in the dot of the letter ba.

On the other hand, if that person claims to see it 
in an unveiling (kashf) by this saying, then he has 
either of these two states:

1. If he claims that he has been informed via un- 
veiling (munkashif) that all the knowledge is 
in two or three letters then he may either know 
it or not know it, but still he may be believed.

2. On the other hand, if he claims that he been 
informed via unveiling that all the knowledge is 
in two or three letters, and also he has studied 
(mutala‘a) it within that two or three letters, 
then his claim is false. He is not credible.
