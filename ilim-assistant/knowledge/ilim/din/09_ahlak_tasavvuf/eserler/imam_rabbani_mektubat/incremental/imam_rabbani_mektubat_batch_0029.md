In the same way that Islam is the opposite of un- 
belief, this world is the opposite of the last world. 
This world and the last world do not unite. This 
world can be given up via two methods:

1. Take up mubah deeds only as much as nec- 
essary and then refrain from the rest [of the 
mubah, and all makruh or haram deeds], which 
is the better method of refraining from this 
world

mat, mutashabihat) deeds only, and enjoy the 
mubah.

8.16.1 What God is like

You may know, and become aware that our God 
(parvardigar) of us and you, instead God of all the 
inhabitants of the cosmos (‘alamiyan), be it the 
skies, or be it the earth, (samawat, aradin) be it 
heavens, or be it the earth, he is one (yek) and 
‘without how’ (bechun va becheguneh).

[God] is blameless from having a likeness or re- 
semblance (shabh, manand) and absolved from be- 
ing a form or shape (shakl, mithal). Being a father 
or a son is not possible for Him. How will it be 
possible for him to have a peer or model (kafa’at,

[God’s] existence has no beginning (bidayat), and 
his ever-abidingness i.e. permanence (baga’) has no 
end (nihayat). Whatever good or perfect (khair,

imperfect or vanishing into extinction (nags, zawal)

deserves to be worshiped (sazawar-i parastish).

should employ your intellect (‘agl,) look far ahead, 
and give up following them.
