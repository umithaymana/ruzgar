Dear sir! After crossing (tayy) the waystations 
of the wayfaring (manazil-i suluk), and cutting

side and inside the nafs (afaqi, anfusi). That ikhlas 
is one-third of the sharia. Sharia comprises three 
parts:

1. knowledge (‘ilm) 
2. practice (‘aml)

3. purity of intention (ckhlas).
