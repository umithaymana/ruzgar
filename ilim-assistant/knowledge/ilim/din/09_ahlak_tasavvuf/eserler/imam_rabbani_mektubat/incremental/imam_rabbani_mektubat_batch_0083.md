Following millat

Question: Following the millat may mean 
following the millat completely (tamam). If 
the meaning is complete compliance, then 
how can it be done when many of the ear- 
lier injunctions have been abrogated? On 
the other hand, if it is partial compliance, 
then even that is not without any unsure- 
ness. Comment?

Answer: The ulama has explained it in 
detail. Look there, because it is the job 
of the zahiri ulama. It has no particular 
relationship with the job of the sufis.

been coming out of me, they are so un- 
known and rare that my peers would see 
me hatefully, and my own people would op- 
pose me, and as the result, earn guilt. Nei- 
ther do I have any choice in attaining those, 
nor do I have any right to publicize those. 
Yes! I have been informed that the first 
ta‘ayyun is ta‘ayyun-i wujudi, and it is the 
origin of entification and nurturer (rabb)
