## parça 80

The bearer of this letter Shaikh Allah Baksh has 
realized a sort of jadhba and love (jadhb, mahab- 
bat). Since he requested for it, I wrote a few lines. 
In summary, he is going there hoping to serve you. 
He had many more purposes, but I refused to make 
specific recommendations, and instead only writing 
that he is going there to meet you. Writing more 
would be lack of adab.
