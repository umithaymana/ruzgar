45 GÖKKÜRE MATHEMATICAL SCIENCES IN THE OTTOMAN REALM AT THE THRESHOLD OF THE AGE OF TRANSFORMATION Khulāṣat al-Hayʾa / خلاصة الهيئة An early copy of the T urkish astronomical treatise translated and adapted by Seydi Ali Reis based on the works of ‘Alī al-Qūshjī, one of the leading figures in Ottoman astronomy and maritime geography. T urkish / Naskh 95 ff., 19 ll. 240x150-160x70 mm. The manuscript is written in Naskh script in black and red inks on cream- coloured, sized Eastern paper. The paper is heterogeneous in texture, and no laid or chain lines could be identified. The copy appears to have been transcribed in 955/1548. The flyleaf bears the statement of Şehzāde Sultan Aḥmed, while folio 1r contains a Harem-i Hümāyūn ownership inscription together with a waqf statement bearing the ṭuġrā of Sultan Maḥmūd I. The manuscript is ruled throughout. The ruling and the diagrams contained in the text are executed in red ink. The binding consists of the upper and lower covers, a sertāb (fore-edge flap), and an miqlab (envelope flap). The exterior is covered in brown leather. The covers are decorated with khatāʾī and rūmī motifs, while the miqlab features a shamsa (medallion) executed in the mülemmaʿ technique with a vegetal composition. The edges of the covers and the envelop flap are framed by gilt zencerek (interlaced chain border) and fillets. Having participated in one of the eastern campaigns of Sultan Süleiman the Magnificent, Seydi Ali Reis, upon his return from the campaign, stayed in Aleppo in the service of Hamdullāh b. Shaykh Jamāl al-Dīn, a descendant of Khvājah ʿAbdallāh Anṣārī, where he benefited from him in the mathematical, astronomical, and philosophical sciences. One day, during a scholarly conversation, his master lamented the scarcity of T urkish works despite the abundance of Arabic and Persian treatises on astronomy and recommended that the astronomical text of ʿAlī Qūshjī be rendered into T urkish. As a result of this encouragement and advice, Seydi Ali Reis composed this work, entitled Khulāṣat al-Hayʾa. The core of Khulāṣat al-Hayʾa is based on the astronomical works of ʿAlī Qūshjī. However, in accordance with the translation practices of the period, the author did not confine himself to transmitting only the content of ʿAlī Qūshjī’s works, he also drew upon Qāḍīzāde’s commentary on the Mulakhkhaṣ and the works of Quṭb al-Dīn Shīrāzī, particularly Nihāyat al- idrāk, as well as several other authoritative books. In addition, he added further material to the text, thereby producing a work that may be regarded as both a translation and an original composition, thus signing his name to a text that can be considered as much authorship as translation. The work consists of muqaddima (introduction) and two main maqāla (book). The muqaddima explains the preliminary topics that must be known before beginning the study of astronomy. The first maqāla, consisting of six chapters, examines the states, positions, and motions of celestial bodies. The second maqāla, comprising twelve chapters, discusses the shape of the Earth, its division into climatic zones, and the reasons for its differences from celestial conditions. TÜYEK, Süleymaniye Manuscript Library, Ayasofya 2591

46 GÖKKÜRE DEĞİŞİMLER ÇAĞI ŞAFAĞINDA OSMANLILAR’DA MATEMATİK BİLİMLERİ Kitâbü’l-Muhît fî İlmi’l-Eflâk ve’l-Ebhur / كتاب المحيط في علم الأفلاك والأبحر Osmanlı bilgini ve denizcisi Seydi Ali Reis’in deniz coğrafyasına ve astronomiye dair Türkçe telif- tercümesinin bezemeli nüshası. Türkçe / Nesih 69 yk., 23 st. 247x149-190x100 mm. Mustafa b. Ahmed tarafından 1077/1666 yılında, İstanbul’da istinsah edilen eser, krem rengi aharlı Avrupa kâğıdına, siyah ve kırmızı mürekkep kullanılarak nesih hatla yazılıdır. Metin reddâdeli olup, 1b ve 2a sayfaları mavi, diğer sayfalar ise kırmızı cetvelle çevrelenmiştir. 1a sayfasında Sultan III. Osman tuğralı vakıf mührü yer almaktadır. 1b sayfasında bulunan unvan bezemesi altın zemin üzerine lacivert ve kırmızı renkte rûmî motifi ile süslemelidir. Nüshanın cildi ön-arka kapak, sertab ve mıklepten oluşmaktadır. Cilt yeşil tekstil ile kaplanmıştır. XVI. yüzyıl Osmanlı bilgini ve denizcisi Seydi Ali Reis bu eseri Hindistan’da iken kaleme almış ve Osmanlı Ülkesi’ne dönüşünde Kânûnî Sultan Süleyman’a sunmuştur. Hint Okyanusu’nda sefere çıkan denizcilerin kılavuz almadan yollarına devam etmelerini sağlamak üzere kaleme alınan eser on bab ve elli fasla ayrılmıştır. Birinci bab yön tayini, gök daireleri, yıldızların uzaklıklarının ve yüksekliklerinin hesaplanması; ikinci bab takvim, güneş ve ay yılları; üçüncü ve dördüncü bablar pusula; beşinci bab denizcilikte önem taşıyan yıldızlar, altıncı bab Hint Okyanusu’ndaki başlıca limanlar ve adalar; yedinci bab astronomi bilgileri ve bazı limanlar arasındaki uzaklıklar; sekizinci bab rüzgârlar, özellikle muson rüzgârlarının çeşitli takvimlere göre başladığı tarihler; dokuzuncu bab izlenecek deniz yolları; onuncu bab tufanlar, büyük fırtınalar ve bunlara karşı alınması gereken önlemler konularını işlemektedir. Seydi Ali Reis kitabına ek olarak bazı haritalar da çizmiş, böylece çalışmasını ayrıca Hint Okyanusu için önemli ve güvenli bir portolan haline getirmiştir. TÜYEK, Nuruosmaniye YEK, Nuruosmaniye 2948

47 GÖKKÜRE MATHEMATICAL SCIENCES IN THE OTTOMAN REALM AT THE THRESHOLD OF THE AGE OF TRANSFORMATION Kitāb al-Muḥīṭ fī ʿIlm al-Aflāk wa-l-Abḥur / كتاب المحيط في علم الأفلاك والأبحر An illuminated copy of an annotated translation in T urkish on maritime geography and astronomy by the Ottoman scholar and navigator Seydi Ali Reis. T urkish / Naskh 69 ff., 23 ll. 247x149-190x100 mm. The manuscript was copied in 1077/1666 in Istanbul by Muṣṭafā b. Aḥmad. It is written in Naskh script in black and red inks on cream-coloured, sized European paper. The text has reddāde (catchwords). Folios 1v and 2r are ruled in blue, while the remaining folios are ruled in red. Folio 1r bears a waqf statement with the ṭuġrā of Sultan ʿUthmān III. On folio 1v a decorated title panel, ornamented with rūmī motifs in blue and red on a gold ground. The binding consists of the upper and lower covers, a sertāb (fore-edge flap) and a miqlab (envelope flap) The binding is covered in green textile. The sixteenth-century Ottoman scholar and navigator Seydi Ali Reis composed this work while he was in India and later presented it to Sultan Süleiman the Magnificent upon his return to the Ottoman lands. The work was written to enable sailors sailing in the Indian Ocean to continue their voyages without the need for navigational guides. It is divided into ten bāb (chapter) and fifty faṣl (section) The first bāb deals with the determination of directions, celestial circles, and the calculation of the distances and altitudes of stars; the second with calendars, solar and lunar years; the third and fourth with the compass; the fifth with the stars significant for navigation; the sixth with the principal ports and islands of the Indian Ocean; the seventh with astronomical information and distances between certain ports; the eighth with winds, particularly the dates of the monsoon winds according to various calendars; the ninth with maritime routes to be followed; and the tenth with storms, great tempests, and the precautions to be taken against them. In addition, Seydi Ali Reis drew several maps in the book, thereby turning his work into an important and reliable portolan for the Indian Ocean. TÜYEK, Nuruosmaniye Manuscript Library, Nuruosmaniye 2948

48 GÖKKÜRE DEĞİŞİMLER ÇAĞI ŞAFAĞINDA OSMANLILAR’DA MATEMATİK BİLİMLERİ Risâle-i Usturlâb-ı Selîmî / رسالهٔٔ أسطرلاب سليمي Takiyyuddin Râsıd’ın selefi müneccimbaşı Mustafa b. Ali el-Muvakkit’in usturlabın çeşitleri ve kullanımını konu alan Türkçe risalesinin nüshası. Türkçe / Nesih 62 yk., 15 st. 145x100-100x55 mm. Eser heterojen krem renkli aharlı filigranlı Avrupa kâğıdı üzerine siyah ve kırmızı mürekkep kullanılarak nesih hatla yazılıdır. 1078/1667 tarihinde istinsah edilen mevcut nüshasının metin kısmı reddâdelidir; başlıklarda kırmızı mürekkep kullanılmıştır. 1a sayfası başta olmak üzere bazı sayfalarında, koleksiyonun sahibi olan Şeyhülislam Veliyyüddin Efendi’nin mührü yer almaktadır. XVI. yüzyıl Osmanlı astronomi bilgini Muslihuddin Mustafa b. Ali el-Muvakkit er-Rûmî (ö. 959/1571) halk arasında daha çok Koca Saatçi lakabıyla tanınmıştır. Uzun süre Sultan Selim Camii’nde muvakkitlik yaptığı için Selîmî nisbesiyle anılmış, 967/1560 yılından itibaren yürüttüğü Müneccimbaşı vazifesine vefatından sonra Takiyyuddin Râsıd tayin edilmiştir. Müellif, 951/1544 yılında usturlap konusunda kaleme aldığı Risâle-i Usturlâb-ı Selîmî’de kendi geliştirdiği bir usturlap türünün kullanımından da bahsetmektedir. Bir mukaddime ile kırk beş bab halinde düzenlenen eser, klasik astronominin en önemli aletlerinden olan usturlabın tarihi açısından büyük önem taşımaktadır. TÜYEK, Beyazıt YEK, Veliyyüddin 2268

49 GÖKKÜRE MATHEMATICAL SCIENCES IN THE OTTOMAN REALM AT THE THRESHOLD OF THE AGE OF TRANSFORMATION Risāla-yi Usturlāb-ı Selīmī / رسالهٔٔ أسطرلاب سليمي A manuscript copy of the T urkish treatise on the types and use of the astrolabe by müneccimbaşı (chief astrologer) Mustafa b. ʿAlī al-Muwaqqit, the predecessor of Taqī al-Dīn al-Rāṣid. T urkish / Naskh 62 ff., 15 ll. 145x100-100x55 mm. The manuscript is written in Naskh script in black and red inks on heterogeneous cream-coloured, sized, watermarked European paper. The extant copy was transcribed in 1078/1667. The main text has reddāde (catchwords). Headings are in red ink. The folio 1r, as well as several other folios, bears the statement of Shaykh al-Islām Walī al-Dīn Efendi, the owner of the collection. The sixteenth-century Ottoman astronomer Muṣliḥ al- Dīn Muṣṭafā b. ʿAlī al-Muwaqqit al-Rūmī (d. 959/1571) was commonly known among the public by the nickname Koca Saatçi (the Great Timekeeper). Having served for a long period as muwaqqit (timekeeper) at the Sultan Selim Mosque, he was also known by the nisba Selīmī. From 967/1560 onwards, he held the office of müneccimbaşı and after his death, Taqī al-Dīn Rāṣid was appointed to this position. In his treatise on the astrolabe, Risāla-yi Usturlāb-ı Selīmī, composed in 951/1544, the author also describes a type of astrolabe that he himself developed. The work, arranged in a preface muqaddima (introduction) and forty-five bāb (chapters) is of great importance for the history of the astrolabe, one of the most significant instruments of classical astronomy. TÜYEK, Beyazıt Manuscript Library, Veliyyüddin 2268
