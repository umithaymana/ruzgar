123 GÖKKÜRE MATHEMATICAL SCIENCES IN THE OTTOMAN REALM AT THE THRESHOLD OF THE AGE OF TRANSFORMATION Risāle fī Samt al-Qibla / رسالة في سمت القبلة An illuminated copy of the Arabic astronomical work on the determination of the qibla direction, composed by ‘Alī al-Qūshjī’s grandson Mīrim Çelebi and presented to Bayezid II. Arabic / Taʿlīq 36 ff., 11 ll. 170x110 - 111x66 mm. The manuscript, which was presumably prepared for the palace library, is written in taʿlīq script using red, black, and gold ink on chickpea-colored, sized Eastern paper. On folio 1v, there is a title piece illumination decorated with khatā’ī and rūmī motifs on a gold and navy blue ground. Within the illuminated area, the basmala is written with white lead. The text is bordered with a gold frame from beginning to end. On folio 2r, a dedication to Bayezid II is mentioned in gold ink. Bordeaux leather was used for the binding of the work. On the front and back covers, shamsa (medallion) and on the miqlab (envelope flap) salbek (pendant) forms were applied with the blind-stamping technique, and place was given to its khatāyī group decoration. It is bordered with a gold frame. The binding of the manuscript consists of front and back covers, a sertab (fore-edge flap) and a miqlab. The exterior of the binding is covered with bordeaux-colored leather. On the covers and the miqlab, there is a shamsa containing a floral composition applied with the blind-stamping technique. The edges of the covers are bordered with a gold frame and a zencerek (interlaced chain border). This work is an Arabic astronomical treatise presented to Bayezid II by Mīrim Çelebi, who was a grandson of Qāḍīzāde-i Rūmī from his paternal side and ‘Alī al-Qūshjī from his maternal side, making him one of the most important mathematicians and astronomers raised in the Ottoman realm. Mīrim Çelebi served as a professor in the madrasas of Gelibolu, Edirne, Bursa, and Istanbul; he was invited to the court where he taught mathematical sciences to Bayezid II, and later served as the military judge of Anatolia during the reign of Selim I. The work, as understood from its title, focuses on the determination of the qibla direction and is a pedagogical text demonstrating this process step by step. The book is organized into a muqaddima (introduction) and five bāb (chapter). In the introduction, Mīrim Çelebi provides the definitions of the concepts to be used; in the first bāb, he shows the way to determine the latitude of the city whose qibla direction is sought. In the second bāb, he describes what he defines as “the easiest” way to find the longitude of the city in question. In the third bāb, he demonstrates the method for determining the measurements of a surface on the Earth; in the fourth bāb, the different ways to find the meridian line and the east-west lines; and in the fifth bāb, based on all these steps, he explains how the qibla direction is to be found. Finally, he applies the qibla determination calculations specifically for Istanbul through the methods he provides. TÜYEK, Süleymaniye Manuscript Library, Ayasofya 2629

Mahmud tarafından vakfedilen müzehheb nüshası. Türkçe / Nesih 59 yk., 15 st. 245x125-140x60 mm. Unvan sayfası XVI. yüzyıl Osmanlı tezhib sanatının özelliklerini taşıyan bu bezemeli nüsha, krem rengi, aharlı Avrupa kâğıdı üzerine siyah ve zer mürekkep kullanılarak nesih hattıyla kaleme alınmıştır. Altın zemin üzerine uygulanan hatâyî ve rûmî motifleriyle süslenen unvan tezhibinin kitabesinde, lacivert zemin üzerine üstübeç tekniğinde Mirkâtü’s-Semâ yazılıdır. Etrafı, altın cetveller ve lacivert ara suyu (ara pervaz) ile çevrelenen bezeme, lacivert renkli tığlarla tamamlanmıştır. Nüshanın cildi ön-arka kapak, sertab ve mıklepten oluşmaktadır. Cildin dışı açık kahverengi deri ile kaplıdır. Kapaklar ve mıklepte altın renkli saadet düğümü motifi bulunmaktadır. Eser, Ali Kuşçu’nun Risâle der İlm-i hey’e adlı Farsça astronomi eserinin Perviz Abdullah Efendi tarafından 934/1527-28 yılında tamamlanan Türkçe çevirisidir. XVI. yüzyıl Osmanlı düşünce hayatının kilit isimlerinden Kemalpaşazâde’nin öğrencisi olan mütercim gerek fıkıh, astronomi, tıp gibi alanlara dair eserleri gerekse ilmî- bürokratik görevleriyle bilinen bir düşünürdür. Risâle der İlm-i hey’e’nin bilinen ilk Türkçe çevirisi olan bu çalışma, giriş (mukaddime) ve iki bölümden (makale) oluşmaktadır. İlk kısımdan (geometri ve doğa felsefesi) oluşan mukaddimede astronomi bilimine başlamadan önce bilinmesi gereken konular ele alınmıştır. Altı bâba ayrılan birinci makalede gök cisimlerinin izahına; on bâbdan oluşan ikinci makalede ise Yer’in şekli, iklimlere bölünmemesi, göksel durumların farklılığının sebebi gibi hususlara yer verilmektedir. 127 GÖKKÜRE MATHEMATICAL SCIENCES IN THE OTTOMAN REALM AT THE THRESHOLD OF THE AGE OF TRANSFORMATION Mirqāt al-Samāʾ / مرقاة السماء An illuminated copy of the T urkish translation titled Mirqāt al-Samāʾ, rendered by the 16th-century Ottoman scholar Perviz Abdullah from ‘Ali al-Qūshjī’s Persian astronomical treatise Risāla dar ʿilm-i hayʾa, endowed by Mahmud I. T urkish / Naskh 59 ff., 15 ll. 245x125 - 140x60 mm. This illuminated manuscript, which bears the characteristics of 16th-century Ottoman illumination art, is written in naskh script using black and gold ink on cream-colored, sized European paper. The headpiece, decorated with khatā’ī and rūmī motifs applied on a gold ground, bears the inscription Mirqāt al-Samāʾ written with white lead technique on a navy blue background. This decorative design, enclosed by golden ruleframes and a navy blue inner panel, is completed with navy blue tīgh (vertical stalks). The binding of the manuscript consists of front and back covers, a sertab (fore-edge flap), and a miqlab (envelope flap). The exterior of the binding is covered with light brown leather. There is a gold-colored endless knot motif on the covers and the miqlab. The work is the T urkish translation, completed in 934/1527-28 by Perviz Abdullah Efendi, of ‘Alī al-Qūshjī’s Persian astronomy treatise titled Risāla dar ʿilm-i hayʾa. The translator, who was a student of Kemalpaşazāde, one of the key figures of 16th-century Ottoman intellectual life, is a thinker known both for his works in fields such as Islamic jurisprudence, astronomy, and medicine, and for his scholarly-bureaucratic duties. This study, which is the first known T urkish translation of Risāla dar ʿilm-i hayʾa, consists of a muqaddima (introduction) and two maqāla (books). In the introduction, which consists of the first part (geometry and natural philosophy), subjects that need to be known before starting the science of astronomy are addressed. In the first book, which is divided into six sections, the explanation of celestial bodies is provided; in the second book, consisting of ten sections, matters such as the shape of the Earth, the impossibility of its division into climates, and the reasons for the divergence of terrestrial conditions from celestial states are included. TÜYEK, Süleymaniye Manuscript Library, Ayasofya 2703

128 GÖKKÜRE DEĞİŞİMLER ÇAĞI ŞAFAĞINDA OSMANLILAR’DA MATEMATİK BİLİMLERİ Fethu’l-Fethiyye / فتح الفتحية Osmanlı astronomi bilgini Gulâm Sinân tarafından hocası Ali Kuşçu’nun er-Risâletü’l-Fethiyye’si üzerine kaleme alınan Fethu’l-Fethiyye isimli eserin Halet Efendi tarafından vakfedilen nüshası. Arapça/Talik 117 yk., 25 st. 208x120-157x58 mm. Eser krem renk aharlı Avrupa kâğıdı üzerine siyah ve kırmızı mürekkep kullanılarak talik hatla yazılıdır. Eserde süsleme yoktur. Nüshanın cildi ön-arka kapak, sertab ve mıklepten oluşmaktadır. Cilt, bordo renkli deri üzerine uygulanmış kırmızı, mavi ve açık yeşil renklerden oluşan bülbül yuvası ebru ile kaplanmıştır. Eser, Ali Kuşçu’nun er-Risâletü’l-Fethiyye adlı Arapça astronomi eserine Gulâm/Köle Sinân adıyla bilinen Sinâneddin Yûsuf tarafından yazılan şerhtir. Fâtih Sultan Mehmed ve Sultan II. Bayezid döneminin âlimleri arasında gösterilen şârih, Sultan II. Murad’ın vezirlerinden birinin kölesi olup adındaki Gulâm ifadesi buradan gelmektedir. Küçük yaştan itibaren iyi bir eğitim almış, Bursa’da Manastır ve Sultâniye, İstanbul’da ise Semâniye medreselerinde müderrislik yapmıştır. Gulâm Sinân şerhin girişinde, Ali Kuşçu’nun bu eserini Fâtih Sultan Mehmed’in Uzun Hasan’a karşı zaferi münasebetiyle el-Fethiyye diye adlandırdığını; kendisinin de Sultan II. Bâyezid zamanında kâfirlerin en güçlüsüne karşı fetihlerin yakın olması hasebiyle şerhe Fethu’l-Fethiyye adını verdiğini belirtmektedir. Kitabın yazılış tarihini ise eserin sonunda 890/1485 olarak kaydetmiştir. Eser, sadece Osmanlı’da kaleme alınmış ilk teorik astronomi eserlerinden birinin şerhi olması bakımından değil, dönemin hoca-öğrenci ilişkisine ve derslerin nasıl işlendiğine dair ayrıntılı tanıklıkları aktarması bakımından da önemlidir. Bilim tarihinde az sayıda örneği olan bu yaklaşım eseri oldukça önemli bir noktaya taşımaktadır. TÜYEK, Süleymaniye YEK, Halet Efendi 537

Arabic / Taʿlīq 117 ff ., 25 ll. 208x120 - 157x58 mm. Th e work is written in taʿlīq script using black and red ink on cream-colored, sized European paper. Th ere is no decoration in the work. Th e binding of the manuscript consists of front and back covers, a sertab (fore-edge fl ap) and a miqlab (envelope fl ap). Th e binding is covered with a bülbül yuvası marbled paper consisting of red, blue, and light green colors applied over bordeaux leather. Th e work is a commentary written by Sinān al-Dīn Yūsuf, known by the name Gulām/Köle Sinān, on ‘Alī al-Qūshjī›s Arabic astronomical treatise titled al-Risāla al-Fathiyya. Th e commentator, who is listed among the scholars of the periods of Mehmed the Conqueror and Bayezid II, was the slave of one of the viziers of Murad II, and the expression Gulām in his name originates from here. Having received a good education from an early age, he served as a professor in Manastır and Sultaniye in Bursa, and the Semaniye madrasas in Istanbul. Gulām Sinān states at the beginning of the commentary that ‘Alī al-Qūshjī named this work al-Fathiyya on the occasion of Mehmed the Conqueror’s victory against Uzun Hasan, and that he himself gave the commentary the title Fath al-Fathiyya due to the proximity of conquests against the most powerful of the infidels during the time of Bayezid II. He recorded the composition date of the book as 890/1485 at the end of the work. Th e work is significant not only because it is a commentary on one of the first theoretical astronomy works composed in the Ottoman realm, but also because it conveys detailed testimonies regarding the master- student relationship of the period and how the courses were conducted. Th is approach, of which there are few examples in the history of science, elevates the work to a highly important position. TÜYEK, Süleymaniye Manuscript Library, Halet Efendi 537
