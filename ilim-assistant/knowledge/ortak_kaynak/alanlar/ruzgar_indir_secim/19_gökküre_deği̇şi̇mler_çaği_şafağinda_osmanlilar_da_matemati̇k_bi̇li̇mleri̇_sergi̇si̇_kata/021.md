107 GÖKKÜRE MATHEMATICAL SCIENCES IN THE OTTOMAN REALM AT THE THRESHOLD OF THE AGE OF TRANSFORMATION Sidrat Muntahā al-Afkār fī Malakūt al-Falak al-Dawwār / سدرة منتهى الأفكار في ملكوت الفلك الدوار A copy copied by T evekkül Babür of the work by Taqī al-Dīn al-Rāṣid, in which he compiled his corrections and additions to the Zīj of Uluğ Bey, dedicated to Şeyhülislam Hoca Sadeddin Efendi. Arabic / Taʿlīq 103 fols., 20 lines. 280x170-200-115 mm. The work is written in taʿlīq script using black and red ink on cream-colored, watermarked machine-made wood-pulp paper. The paper has a homogeneous structure. Geometric forms drawn with black ink are located at the end of the work. The headings in the copy, which was copied in 1939, are in red ink. Sidrat muntahā al-afkār fī malakūt al-falak al-dawwār was authored with the aim of correcting and completing the Zīj of Uluğ Bey. Furthermore, it is a significant astronomical work that presents the results of the observations the author conducted in Istanbul and Egypt. The work, which begins with the definition and calculation of trigonometric lines, draws a comprehensive theoretical framework encompassing astronomical clocks, the intersection of celestial circles, observation instruments, and observation methods for the movements of the sun and the moon. In the work, which demonstrates Taqī al-Dīn al-Rāṣid’s innovative approach to trigonometry and practical astronomy, Jamshīd al-Kāshī’s studies on the value of sine were advanced to a further point and in the calculation of solar parameters, findings congruent with modern astronomy were obtained by utilizing the “three observation points method” also used by Tycho Brahe. The author, who abandoned the use of the moon as an intermediary in the longitude calculations of fixed stars and brought direct observation methods to the forefront, aims to minimize observation error with this work. The work, which is considered an incomplete study due to the absence of a conclusion, is regarded as one of the most qualified indicators of Taqī al-Dīn al-Rāṣid’s effort to achieve mathematical precision and to solidify observation practice with theoretical foundations, even though it has not fully achieved the fame it deserves. Another factor that increases the value of the manuscript is that the copy was copied by T evekkül Babür (d. 1979), a T urkestani intellectual from Tashkent who was raised in reformist circles, migrated from T urkestan to T urkey following Soviet hegemony, was active among T urkestani emigrants and in the T urkestan national struggle, and worked at the Kandilli Observatory for a period. 108 GÖKKÜRE DEĞİŞİMLER ÇAĞI ŞAFAĞINDA OSMANLILAR’DA MATEMATİK BİLİMLERİ Cerîdetü’t-Dürer ve Harîdetü’l-Fiker / جريدة الدرر وخريدة الفكر Takiyyuddin Râsıd’ın ilk defa ondalık kesirleri astronomi hesaplarında kullanarak hazırladığı zîcin müellif nüshası. Arapça / Talik 75 yk., 18 st. 180x70-130x50 mm. Eser krem renkli aharlı Avrupa kâğıdı üzerine siyah ve kırmızı mürekkep kullanılarak talik hatla yazılıdır. Ferağ kaydından 990/1582 tarihinde Takiyyuddin Râsıd tarafından kaleme alındığı anlaşılıyor olsa da yazı karakterine bakılarak müellif gözetiminde hazırlanan nüsha demek daha isabetlidir. Nüshanın başında eserin fihristi yer almaktadır. Başlıklar ve tablolar kırmızı mürekkeple belirtilmiştir. Nüshanın cildi ön-arka kapaktan oluşmaktadır. Cildin dışı koyu kahverengi deri ile kaplıdır. Kapaklarda altın şemse ve salbekler içerisinde saadet düğümü motifi bulunmaktadır. Kapakların kenarları altın cetvel ile çevrelenmiştir. Takiyyuddin Râsıd’ın ondalık kesirleri astronomi hesaplamalarında kullanarak trigonometri cetvellerini tertip ettiği bu küçük boyutlu zîc, ünlü bilim tarihçimiz Salih Zeki tarafından matematik tarihinde “Türkler ve Müslümanlar için iftihar kaynağı” olarak nitelenen önemli bir buluşunu ihtiva etmektedir. İslam dünyasında ondalık kesirlerle problem çözümüne dair ilk örnekleri Gıyâsuddin Cemşid el-Kâşî Miftâhu’l-hisâb adlı eserinde vermiş olsa da bu kesirlerin trigonometri cetvellerine uygulanması ilk defa Takiyyuddin Râsıd tarafından gerçekleştirilmiştir. Takiyyuddin Râsıd, Cemşid Kâşî’nin ondalık kesirleri astronomide kullanma girişimini takdirle anmakla birlikte, bu yöntemi bir çözüm modeline dönüştürüp uygulamalı bir zîc haline getirme şerefinin kendisine ait olduğunu ifade etmektedir. Ondalık kesirler kullanarak hazırladığı cetvellerin başına “Takiyyuddin’in icadı” ibaresini eklemek suretiyle kendi katkısını belirtmiştir. Takiyyuddin Râsıd tarafından Şeyhülislam Sadeddin Efendi’ye ithaf edilen eser, ondalık sistemin astronomik hesaplamalara entegre edilmesi bakımından döneminin oldukça ilerisinde ve özgün bir bilimsel katkı sunmaktadır. 109 GÖKKÜRE MATHEMATICAL SCIENCES IN THE OTTOMAN REALM AT THE THRESHOLD OF THE AGE OF TRANSFORMATION Jarīdat al-Durar wa-Kharīdat al-Fikar / جريدة الدرر وخريدة الفكر Th e author’s autograph copy of the zīj (astronomical table) prepared by Taqī al-Dīn al-Rāṣid, in which decimal fractions were used in astronomical calculations for the first time. Arabic / Ta‘līq 75 leaves (ff .), 18 lines (ll.). 180x70-130x50 mm. Th e work is written in ta‘līq script using black and red ink on cream- colored, sized European paper. Although the colophon indicates that it was penned by Taqī al-Dīn al-Rāṣid himself in 990/1582, considering the handwriting characteristics, it is more accurate to describe it as a copy prepared under the author’s direct supervision. Th ere is an index of the work at the beginning of the manuscript. Headings and tables are indicated in red ink. Th e binding consists of front and back covers. Th e exterior of the binding is covered with dark brown leather. Th e covers feature a shamsa (medallion) with salbek (pendants) containing an endless motif. Th e edges of the covers are bordered with a gold frame. Th is small-scale zīj, in which Taqī al-Dīn al-Rāṣid compiled trigonometry tables by utilizing decimal fractions in astronomical calculations, contains an important discovery characterized by our famous historian of science, Salih Zeki, as a “source of pride for T urks and Muslims” in the history of mathematics. Although the fi rst examples of problem-solving with decimal fractions in the Islamic world were given by Ghiyāth al-Dīn Jamshīd al- Kāshī in his work titled Miftāḥ al-ḥisāb, the application of these fractions to trigonometry tables was performed for the fi rst time by Taqī al-Dīn al-Rāṣid. While acknowledging with appreciation al-Kāshī’s initiative to use decimal fractions in astronomy, Taqī al-Dīn al-Rāṣid states that the honor of transforming this method into a solution model and turning it into an applied zīj belongs to himself. He highlighted his own contribution by adding the phrase “the invention of Taqī al- Dīn” at the beginning of the tables he prepared using decimal fractions. Dedicated by Taqī al- Dīn al-Rāṣid to Şeyhülislam Sadeddin Efendi, the work presents a highly advanced and original scientifi c contribution for its period in terms of integrating the decimal system into astronomical calculations.
