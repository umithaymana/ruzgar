Desired Articles | Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy Search The Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy encourages any philosophy professor with a Ph.D. or equivalent degree from around the world to submit a new article. For ideas for article titles, you can consult either our short and long lists of desired articles or our many lists of desired articles by subject-area of philosophy. You may also browse our list of article titles that are currently reserved . Prospective authors may propose an article on any other traditional philosophy subject or philosopher not currently in the IEP. Avoid very narrow topics that are more appropriate for philosophy journal articles. To offer to submit a new article, send an email to a specific subject-area editor or to the general editors with a description of your article, and please indicate: The provisional title of the proposed article, Your educational credentials and university affiliation, A brief comment on your qualifications such as having published or taught courses on the topic, A tentative (and realistic) date of completion; six months is typical, but it must be less than a year.
