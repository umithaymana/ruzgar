Wendy Donner, “Mill’s Utilitarianism” in John Skorupski, ed. The Cambridge Companion to Mill . Cambridge University Press, 1998, 255–92. A discussion of Mill’s views and some recent interpretations of them. David Lyons. Rights, Welfare, and Mill’s Moral Theory. Oxford, 1994. In this series of papers, Lyons defends Mill’s view of morality against some critics, differentiates Mill’s views from both act and rule utilitarianism, and criticizes Mill’s attempt to show that utilitarianism can account for justice. e.
