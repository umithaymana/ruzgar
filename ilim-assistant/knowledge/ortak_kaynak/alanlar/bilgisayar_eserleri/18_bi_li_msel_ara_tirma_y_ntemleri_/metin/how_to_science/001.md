News OSF Changes: A Note to Users Research Predicting Replicability Challenge: Round 3 Results Initiative A Cross-Sector Strategy for Research Data Infrastructure The Open Science Framework (OSF) is Changing OSF is moving toward a more focused role in supporting open science across the research lifecycle. As open research infrastructure has matured, COS is focusing OSF on the areas where it can provide distinctive value and narrowing its scope to one that COS can sustain over the long term. This means phasing out OSF Projects as an active collaboration and storage workspace. Going forward, OSF will focus on study planning and preregistration, community-run preprint services, and linking research records across systems. About the Center for Open Science We envision a future scholarly community in which the process, content, and outcomes of research are openly accessible by default. Research Software Tools We maintain the Open Science Framework (OSF) to help researchers conduct research more rigorously, and manage and share their work more openly. Community Action and Culture Change From individuals to institutions, we educate, train, and support research communities and their affiliates toward greater adoption of open and reproducible practices. Metascience and Research We conduct research on research practices to understand areas of inefficiency, and to evaluate interventions to improve. Lifecycle Open Science Research is more than a publication. Lifecycle Open Science makes the full path of research easier to follow by linking what was planned, what was produced, and what was found — even when those parts live in different places. When the research record is linked and findable, it becomes easier to understand, evaluate, reuse, and build on. &nbsp; Conduct your own open science Register Your Research Create a time-stamped registration of any OSF project, or begin a preregistration prior to your study to increase the transparency and quality of your research. Link Your Research Record Link your study plans and preprints to related data, materials, code, publications, and other results stored elsewhere, creating a clearer research record across systems. Share a Paper or Preprint Sharing a paper or preprint on OSF accelerates scholarly communication, feedback that can improve the work, and discoverability of research. It only takes a few minutes.
