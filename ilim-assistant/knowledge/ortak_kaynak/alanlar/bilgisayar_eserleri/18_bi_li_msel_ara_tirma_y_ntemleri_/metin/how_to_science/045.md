Author Identification ORCID (Open Research and Contributor Identifier) provides researchers with a unique persistent digital identifier which ensures accurate attribution of their scholarly work. By distinguishing researchers from one another, ORCID enables reliable links between individuals and their scholarly outputs. PLOS participates in the auto‑update feature implemented by Crossref such that when a paper is published, the authors’ ORCIDs are deposited and subsequently used to update each author’s ORCID record. To enjoy the full benefits of ORCID integration, we recommend that you authorize Crossref to automatically update your ORCID profile as new articles are published. This ensures that your PLOS articles, as well as the articles you publish with other Crossref-member-publishers, appear on your ORCID profile as soon as they are available online, without you having to make manual updates. When Crossref receives a citation with your ORCID, you'll receive a message in your ORCID inbox with the subject line "Add your published work(s) to your ORCID record". Click the "Grant permissions" button to authorize Crossref to write to your ORCID profile. You'll have the option to authorize the update in this single instance or indefinitely. We recommend choosing indefinitely, so that your ORCID profile is always as complete and up-to-date as possible. Capturing ORCID iDs within Editorial Manager offers many advantages, including more accurate metadata for funding information, streamlined login with ORCID single sign-on and author attribution. PLOS endorses ORCID and requires that all corresponding authors provide an ORCID iD when submitting a manuscript. We also encourage all co-authors to register and use an ORCID iD ORCID Requirement and Deadline&nbsp; The corresponding author must provide an ORCID iD upon submission and ensure it is authenticated in Editorial Manager by the time of Final Acceptance. All co-authors who wish to have their ORCID iD included in the manuscript must link it to the article via the ‘co-author confirmation’ email they receive after submission. This must be completed before final acceptance. All authenticated ORCID iDs provided by authors will be published alongside the article, if accepted. Any ORCIDs that remain unauthenticated by acceptance will be removed from the manuscript and will not be published. We cannot add ORCID iDs after acceptance, for example during the proofing process. Only the individual author can complete the authentication step; PLOS staff or the corresponding author cannot verify ORCID iDs on behalf of authors or their co-authors.&nbsp; How ORCID Information Is Used&nbsp; If the manuscript is accepted:&nbsp; PLOS will publish the corresponding author’s ORCID iD, as well as any ORCID iDs provided by co-authors.&nbsp; PLOS participates in the auto‑update feature implemented by Crossref such that when a paper is published, the authors’ ORCIDs are deposited and subsequently used to update each author’s ORCID record&nbsp; How to Create and Link an ORCID Authors who do not yet have an ORCID iD can register for one in under a minute through ORCID , where you’ll be guided through the registration process.&nbsp; As a corresponding author there are two ways to register and link your ORCID in Editorial Manager.&nbsp; Starting from the Editorial Manager sign in screen, click the green iD button. This will open the ORCID pop up window. If you already have an ORCID iD, enter your ID number or email and your password in the spaces provided and select "Authorize". If you do not yet have an ORCID , choose "Register now" and complete the short registration form. Once you have finished, sign in to Editorial Manager as usual using your existing username and password. Your ORCID is now linked to your profile in the submission system. Going forward, your ORCID iD will be automatically attached to any manuscripts you submit as a corresponding author.&nbsp; Alternatively, you can manually enter your ORCID into your Editorial Manager profile page. Select the "Update my Information" link located in the top left hand corner of the main menu to open your profile. In the "Personal Information" section of your profile page, click "Fetch/Register" link that appears alongside the ORCID field. This will open the ORCID popup window, where you can either authorize an existing account, or register for an account.&nbsp; Co-authors can add their ORCID iD to a submission through the Co-author Confirmation Notice email that is sent after their manuscript has been submitted. In the verification email, the verify authorship link will take them to the Verify Contributing Authorship Page in their Editorial Manager Account. From here, co-authors can click Link to ORCID Record which brings them to the ORCID website to create or verify their ORCID iD. Authors must then go back to click the Submit to Publication link to complete authorship confirmation and link their ORCID iD. Editor and Reviewer Requirements Editors should be aware of the author list and author affiliations when they invite reviewers to review a manuscript in order to reduce the likelihood of inviting individuals with potentially competing interests that would disqualify them from participating in the peer review process. Editors and reviewers should contact the journal with any concerns about the author list or if they identify any potentially competing interests that should be declared or that mean they should recuse themselves from the process.
