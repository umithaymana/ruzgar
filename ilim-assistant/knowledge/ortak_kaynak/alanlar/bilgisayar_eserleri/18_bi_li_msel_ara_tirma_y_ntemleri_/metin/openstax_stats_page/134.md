Forty percent of the smartphone users from 13 to 55+ are at least what age? Solution a. IQR = Q 3 – Q 1 Calculate Q 3 = 75 th percentile and Q 1 = 25 th percentile. invNorm(0.75,36.9,13.9) = Q 3 = 46.2754 invNorm(0.25,36.9,13.9) = Q 1 = 27.5246 IQR = Q 3 – Q 1 = 18.8 b. Find k where P ( x ≥ k ) = 0.40 ("At least" translates to "greater than or equal to.") 0.40 = the area to the right. Area to the left = 1 – 0.40 = 0.60.
