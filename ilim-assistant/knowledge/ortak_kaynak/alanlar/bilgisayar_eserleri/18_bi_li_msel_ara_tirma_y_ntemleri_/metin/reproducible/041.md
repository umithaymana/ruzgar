Stage/Gate (Phase Gate) Product development, large-scale engineering projects Stage completion, Risk management Go/No-go decisions at each phase, Risk mitigation Moderately suitable for research projects that are large and complex, requiring systematic review at each phase. Agile Stage Gate R&amp;D, product development Agility with structured reviews Agile flexibility with stage-wise decision making Highly suitable for research, combining the flexibility of Agile with the structured review process of Stage Gate.
