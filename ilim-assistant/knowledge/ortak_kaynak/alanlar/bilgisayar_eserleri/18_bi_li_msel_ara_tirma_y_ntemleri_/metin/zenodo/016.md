Coverage broadens to further metadata fields, and curation checks start considering file contents alongside metadata. Development Peer Community In and COAR Notify Integration with Peer Community In using the COAR Notify protocol, to display peer review information on Zenodo records. Development EOSC EU Node AAI integration Authentication via the EOSC EU Node AAI. New user accounts are validated automatically using AAI attributes. DevOps Infrastructure enhancements for AI traffic Infrastructure changes to absorb the surge of traffic from AI harvesters without degrading service for human users. Near-term What we plan working on next Development GitLab.com integration Archival of software repositories from GitLab.com, alongside the existing GitHub.com integration. Projects AIRDEC: Advanced AI-assisted metadata and curation Further AI-assisted automation: linking metadata to authoritative identifiers (authors to ORCID, affiliations and funders to ROR), deriving metadata from record context (e.g. subject classification from abstracts), and reviewing records against repository and community curation policies. Future What we investigate Projects EU Open Research Repository: Enhanced search and discovery Better search and discovery in the EU Open Research Repository, with stronger ranking, richer filters and facets, and ways to surface related records. Projects EU Open Research Repository: FAIR and AI-ready datasets FAIRness improvements and AI-ready datasets in the EU Open Research Repository, with metadata, formats, and access patterns suited to downstream AI tooling. Development Researcher profile page A dedicated profile page for selected researchers, displaying their publications, affiliations, competencies and research interests. Our roadmap helps us share what we are working on next and the direction that we're taking Zenodo. The roadmap is updated quarterly in January, April, July and October. Last update: August 20, 2026 Completed 2026 Development Enhanced comments system We enhanced Zenodo's commenting system for submission reviews with several new features: LaTeX equation previews, quote functionality, direct comment replies, local draft autosave, comment collapsing, permalinks, and file attachments. Completed April 2026 Projects EU Open Research Repository: technical specifications As part of the Horizon-Zen Plus project, we published the first technical specifications for the new features for the EU Open Research Repository. They include the descriptions of the planned functionalities and UI mock-ups. Completed February 2026 Projects AIRDEC: Internal launch Internal launch of AI-assisted submission and curation for a limited group of users. Initial scope covers basic metadata fields (title, description, publication date, DOI). Projects EU Open Research Repository: Onboarding automation Automated onboarding of EU-funded project communities into the EU Open Research Repository, with AI-assisted checks on funding metadata. Projects AIRDEC: First milestone As part of the AIRDEC project, we achieved the first milestone which included setting up the initial infrastructure and beginning the development of AI-suggested metadata enhancements for research records. Completed March 2026 2025 Projects Biodiversity Literature Repository branded community The concepts developed for the EU Open Research Repository is being developed and tested now the Biodiversity Literature Repository, which now has a branded portal on http://zenodo.org/communities/biosyslit/ Projects EU Open Research Repository: Automated checks The EU Open Research Repository will be expanded with improved curation capabilities through automated curation checks for Horizon Europe open science compliance. Learn more about the planned features in HORIZON-ZEN FAIR-enabling deposit workflow design Completed March 2025 Development Improved self-service support Currently, there's a number of actions that users need to contact our helpdesk in order to achieve. This includes for instance record deletions, quota increase requests and other standard support requests. We'll be focusing on improving self-service support which should ensure an improved user experience and faster replies, as well as reduced load on our supporters (we're currently handling 500-600 requests a month). Development FAIR improvements We will be improving overall FAIRness of Zenodo. This will also include support for Medical Subject Headings (MeSH) as well as some smaller improvements for marine science. Completed March 2025 2024 Projects Automated detection of EU-funded records We will be working on a workflow for automatic detection and quality assurance of EU-funded records so that they can be included into the EU Open Research Repository. Planned release in December 2024 Development Spam fighting improvements We will be implementing several new spam mitigation measures to keep up with new changes in methods by spammers. Planned November 2024 Development Software improvements and Software Heritage integration As part of the FAIRCORE4EOSC project, we will be improving the support for software source code types in Zenodo, as well as integrate Zenodo with Software Heritage Improvements are right now being tested on sandbox.zenodo.org Development Sharing with users We will add support for sharing records and drafts with users. See docs . Development Vocabularies (ROR, ORCID, MeSH) We will be working on performing regular updates of some of our backend vocabularies such as ROR (funders/affiliations), ORCID (names) and subject vocabularies (EuroSciVoc, Medical Subject Headings). Planned July 2024 Projects EU Open Research Repository The EU Open Research Repository will exit the pilot phase during autumn 2024. This will include improved browse capabilities and onboarding of EU-funded projects. Planned September 2024
