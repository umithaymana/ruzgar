(cl-mec-hybrid-collab)= # Hybrid Collaboration Hybrid collaboration is a way of working where individuals collaborate from a place that they are most comfortable to work from - it be could remote, in-person, or through co-working spaces. This brings in more flexibility for individuals to choose from. The COVID-19 pandemic brought in a paradigm shift in how individuals have been working. Recently, hybrid collaboration models are gaining popularity.
