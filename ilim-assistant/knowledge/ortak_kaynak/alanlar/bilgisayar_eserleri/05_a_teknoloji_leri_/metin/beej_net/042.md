In a nutshell, “block” is techie jargon for “sleep”. You probably noticed that when you run listener , above, it just sits there until a packet arrives. What happened is that it called recvfrom() , there was no data, and so recvfrom() is said to “block” (that is, sleep there) until some data arrives. Lots of functions block. accept() blocks.
