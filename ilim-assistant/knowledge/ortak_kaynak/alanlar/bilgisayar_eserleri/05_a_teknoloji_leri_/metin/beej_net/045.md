The reason I have the master set is that select() actually changes the set you pass into it to reflect which sockets are ready to read. Since I have to keep track of the connections from one call of select() to the next, I must store these safely away somewhere. That is, you want it to send 512 bytes, but it returns 412. What happened to the remaining 100 bytes? Well, they’re still in your little buffer waiting to be sent out. Due to circumstances beyond your control, the kernel decided not to send all the data out in one chunk, and now, my friend, it’s up to you to get the data out there.
