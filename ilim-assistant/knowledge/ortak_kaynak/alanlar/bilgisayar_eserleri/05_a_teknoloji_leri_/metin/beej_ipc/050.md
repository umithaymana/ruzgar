In order to do this, simply add “ -lnsl -lsocket -lresolv ” to the end of the compile command, like so: $ cc -o server server.c -lnsl -lsocket -lresolv If you still get errors, you could try further adding a -lxnet to the end of that command line. I don’t know what that does, exactly, but some people seem to need it. Another place that you might find problems is in the call to setsockopt() .
