If the box’s direction is DOWNRIGHT or UPRIGHT , you want to increase the left attribute. If the direction is DOWNLEFT or UPLEFT , you want to decrease the left attribute. You can try commenting out the time.sleep(0.02) line and running the program to see what it looks like.
