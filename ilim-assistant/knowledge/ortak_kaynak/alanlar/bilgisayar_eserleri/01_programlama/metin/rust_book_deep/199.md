Vec may use any or none of the following strategies, depending on the supplied iterator: preallocate based on Iterator::size_hint() and panic if the number of items is outside the provided lower/upper bounds use an amortized growth strategy similar to pushing one item at a time perform the iteration in-place on the original allocation backing the iterator The last case warrants some attention. It is an optimization that in many cases reduces peak memory consumption and improves cache locality.
