Once route is enabled, every request matching the url pattern will stall unless it's continued, fulfilled or aborted. :::note [`method: BrowserContext.route`] will not intercept requests intercepted by Service Worker. See [this](https://github.com/microsoft/playwright/issues/1090) issue.
