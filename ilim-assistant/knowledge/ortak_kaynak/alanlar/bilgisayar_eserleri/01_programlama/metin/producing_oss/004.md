If your name should be here but is not, please accept my apologies (and let me know, because we can at least fix the online copy). Andy Oram of O'Reilly Media once again went above and beyond the call of duty as an editor. He read closely and made many excellent recommendations; his expertise both in expository writing in general and in open source in particular were apparent in all his comments. I can't thank him enough, and the book is much improved for his attention. Despite having a busy job&#8212;I know firsthand, because we're business partners&#8212;and young children at home, he unhesitatingly volunteered to read through the manuscript and provide feedback.
