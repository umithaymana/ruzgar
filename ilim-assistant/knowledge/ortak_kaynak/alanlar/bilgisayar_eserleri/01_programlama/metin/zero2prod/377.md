However, if you call [`method: WebSocketRoute.onMessage`] on the original route, messages from the page to the server **will not be forwarded** anymore, but should instead be handled by the [`param: WebSocketRoute.onMessage.handler`]. Similarly, calling [`method: WebSocketRoute.onMessage`] on the server-side WebSocket will **stop forwarding messages** from the server to the page, and [`param: WebSocketRoute.onMessage.handler`] should take care of them.
