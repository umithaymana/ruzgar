Angular is a web framework that empowers developers to build fast, reliable applications that users love. Maintained by a dedicated team at Google, Angular provides a broad suite of tools, APIs, and libraries to simplify and streamline your development workflow. Angular gives you a solid platform on which to build fast, reliable applications that scale with both the size of your team and the size of your codebase. Angular.dev is the official home for Angular documentation. Want to see some code?
