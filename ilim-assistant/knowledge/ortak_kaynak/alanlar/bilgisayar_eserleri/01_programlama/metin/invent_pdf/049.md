Summary Creating a program with AI comes down to carefully considering all the possible situations the AI can encounter and how it should respond in each of those situations. The Tic-Tac-Toe AI is simple because not as many moves are possible in Tic-Tac-Toe as in a game like chess or checkers. Our computer AI checks for any possible winning moves. Otherwise, it checks whether it must block the player’s move. Then the AI simply chooses any available corner space, then the center space, then the side spaces. This is a simple algorithm for the computer to follow. The key to implementing our AI is to make copies of the board data and simulate moves on the copy. That way, the AI code can see whether a move results in a win or loss. Then the AI can make that move on the real board.
