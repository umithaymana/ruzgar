Incomplete information [ edit ] Repeated games can include some incomplete information. Repeated games with incomplete information were pioneered by Aumann and Maschler . [ 4 ] While it is easier to treat a situation where one player is informed and the other not, and when information received by each player is independent, it is possible to deal with zero-sum games with incomplete information on both sides and signals that are not independent.
