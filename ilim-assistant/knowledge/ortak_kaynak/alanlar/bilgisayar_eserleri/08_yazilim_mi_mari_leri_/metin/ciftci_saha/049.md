Download XLSX Download CSV API URL ( Open in new tab , copy this URL into your application, or paste an API URL to restore its settings) Data Sources This API uses global UKMO Global 10 km weather forecasts and combines them with high-resolution UKV 2 km model for the United Kingdom and Ireland. Information about UKMO weather models is available here . For UKMO Global, values are interpolated from 3-hourly to 1-hourly after 54 hours and from 6-hourly data after 144 hours. Note: UKMO open-data has an additional delay of 4 hours. The forecast is therefore not as accurate as it could be. You can find the update timings in the model updates documentation .
