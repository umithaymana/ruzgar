RRFS, the Rapid Refresh Forecast System, updates every 6 hours and provides 15-minutely data for CONUS with hourly updates. High-resolution data are only available for North America. For other locations, only GFS is used. For GFS, values are interpolated from 3-hourly to 1-hourly after 120 hours.
