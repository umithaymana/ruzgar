Your signals work the same way as built-in ones: they appear in the Signals tab and you can connect to them like any other. To emit a signal in your scripts, call emit() on the signal. Other nodes can connect to individual signals and react to selected events. Signals have many uses. With them, you can react to a node entering or exiting the game world, to a collision, to a character entering or leaving an area, to an element of the interface changing size, and much more. For example, an Area2D representing a coin emits a body_entered signal whenever the player's physics body enters its collision shape, allowing you to know when the player collected it.
