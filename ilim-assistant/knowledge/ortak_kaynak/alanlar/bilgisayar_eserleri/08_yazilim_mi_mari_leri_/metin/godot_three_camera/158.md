To do so, we'll connect our VisibleOnScreenNotifier3D node's screen_exited signal to the Mob . Select the VisibleOnScreenNotifier3D node and on the right side of the interface, navigate to the Signals dock. Double-click the screen_exited() signal. Connect the signal to the Mob This will add a new function for you in your mob script, _on_visible_on_screen_notifier_3d_screen_exited() . From it, call the queue_free() method. This function destroys the instance it's called on.
