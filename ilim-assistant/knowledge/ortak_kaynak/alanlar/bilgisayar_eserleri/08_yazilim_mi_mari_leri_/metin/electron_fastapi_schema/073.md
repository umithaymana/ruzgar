# ClipboardBookmark Object * `title` string - The title of the bookmark. * `url` string - The URL of the bookmark. A `ClipboardBookmark` is the payload used by the `electron application/bookmark` clipboard custom format. It is passed to [`clipboard.write()`](../clipboard.md#clipboardwritedata) as a [`ClipboardItem`](../clipboard-item.md) `data` value, and is what `getType('electron application/bookmark')` resolves to when reading via [`clipboard.read()`](../clipboard.md#clipboardread).
