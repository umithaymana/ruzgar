MercatorCoordinate uses the web mercator projection ( EPSG:3857 ) with slightly different units: the size of 1 unit is the width of the projected world instead of the "mercator meter" the origin of the coordinate space is at the north-west corner instead of the middle For example, MercatorCoordinate(0, 0, 0) is the north-west corner of the mercator world and MercatorCoordinate(1, 1, 0) is the south-east corner. If you are familiar with vector tiles it may be helpful to think of the coordinate space as the 0/0/0 tile with an extent of 1 . The z dimension of MercatorCoordinate is conformal. A cube in the mercator coordinate space would be rendered as a cube.
