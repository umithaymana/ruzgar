Daboo Standards Track [Page 131] RFC 5546 iTIP December 2009 Appendix A . Differences from RFC 2446 A.1 . Changed Restrictions This specification now defines an allowed combination of &quot;REQUEST- STATUS&quot; codes when multiple iCalendar components are included in an iTIP message. This specification now restricts &quot;RECURRENCE-ID&quot; to only a single occurrence in any one iCalendar component in an iTIP message, as required by [ RFC5545 ]. Changed the &quot;RECURRENCE-ID&quot; entry in the component restriction table to &quot;0 or 1&quot; from &quot;0+&quot;, to fall in line with [ RFC5545 ]. Changed the &quot;FREEBUSY&quot; entry in the &quot;VFREEBUSY&quot;, &quot;PUBLISH&quot;, and &quot;REPLY&quot; restriction tables to &quot;0+&quot; from &quot;1+&quot;, to fall in line with [ RFC5545 ]. Changed the &quot;FREEBUSY&quot; description in the &quot;VFREEBUSY&quot; and &quot;REPLY&quot; restriction tables to indicate that different &quot;FBTYPE&quot; ranges MUST NOT overlap. Changed the &quot;TZNAME&quot; entry in the &quot;VTIMEZONE&quot; restriction table to &quot;0+&quot; from &quot;0 or 1&quot;, to fall in line with [ RFC5545 ]. Changed the &quot;COMMENT&quot; entry in the component restriction tables to &quot;0+&quot; from &quot;0 or 1&quot;, to fall in line with [ RFC5545 ]. Added the &quot;ATTENDEE&quot; entry in the &quot;VALARM&quot; restriction table to match the email alarm type in [ RFC5545 ]. Changed the &quot;CATEGORIES&quot; entry in the component restriction tables to &quot;0+&quot; from &quot;0 or 1&quot;, to fall in line with [ RFC5545 ]. Changed the &quot;RESOURCES&quot; entry in the component restriction tables to &quot;0+&quot; from &quot;0 or 1&quot;, to fall in line with [ RFC5545 ]. Changed the &quot;CONTACT&quot; entry in the &quot;VFREEBUSY&quot; restriction table to &quot;0 or 1&quot; from &quot;0+&quot;, to fall in line with [ RFC5545 ]. Changed the &quot;UID&quot; entry in the &quot;VFREEBUSY&quot; and &quot;PUBLISH&quot; restriction tables to &quot;1&quot; from &quot;0&quot;, to fall in line with [ RFC5545 ]. Added the &quot;COMPLETED&quot; entry in the &quot;VTODO&quot; restriction tables to fall in line with [ RFC5545 ].
