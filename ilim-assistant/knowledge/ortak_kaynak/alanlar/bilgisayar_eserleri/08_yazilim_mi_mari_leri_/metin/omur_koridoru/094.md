The &quot;DTSTART&quot; date coincides with the first instance of the &quot;RRULE&quot; property. The recurring meeting is defined in a particular time zone, presumably that of the originator. The client for each &quot;Attendee&quot; has the responsibility of determining the recurrence time in the &quot;Attendee&#x27;s&quot; time zone. The repeating event starts on Tuesday, July 1, 1997 at 2:00pm PDT (UTC-7). &quot;Attendee&quot; B@example.fr is in France, where the local time on this date is 9 hours ahead of PDT, or 23:00 CEST (UTC+2). &quot;Attendee&quot; C@example.jp is in Japan, where local time is 16 hours ahead of PDT, or Wednesday, July 2 at 06:00 JST (UTC+9). The event repeats weekly on Tuesdays (in PST/PDT). The &quot;RRULE&quot; property results in 20 instances. The last instance falls on Tuesday, November 11, 1997 2:00pm PST. The &quot;RDATE&quot; property adds another instance: WED, 10-SEP-1997 2:00 PM PDT. There are also two exception dates to the recurrence rule. The first one is: o TUE, 09-SEP-1997 14:00 PDT (UTC-7) o TUE, 09-SEP-1997 23:00 CEST (UTC+2) o WED, 10-SEP-1997 06:00 JST (UTC+9) and the second is: o TUE, 28-OCT-1997 14:00 PST (UTC-8) o TUE, 28-OCT-1997 23:00 CET (UTC+1) o WED, 29-OCT-1997 07:00 JST (UTC+9) 4.4.2 .
