The x-axis is labeled \(v(x)\) and goes from &minus;100 to 100; the left side is labeled &lsquo;losses&rsquo; and the right side &lsquo;gains&rsquo;. The y-axis is labeled x and goes from &minus;30 to 30. A single curved line starts at the lower left (approx. (&minus;100,&minus;24)) at about a 30 degree angle, goes through the origin at almost a 90 degree angle then goes back to a 30 degree angle by the end (approx. (100,10)). Graph (b) is a 10 by 10 layout with x and y both going from 0 to 1. The y-axis is labeled \(w(p)\) and the x-x-axis p . A straight dashed line goes from (0,0) to (1,1). A solid curved line also goes between the same points. It is above the dashed line from (0,0) to about (.35,.35) and below the dashed line after that point. Figure 2 description The center of the mostly symetrical diagram consists of four vertical boxes, the whole labeled above as &lsquo;cues&rsquo; and individually inside from top to bottom as \(X_1\), \(X_i\), \(X_j\), \(X_n\). Cue \(X_i\) and \(X_j\) are connected by a line labeled \(\rho_{X_i, X_j}\). To the left of the cues is a box labeled above in green as &lsquo;criterion value&rsquo; and inside as \(Y_e\). Each of the four cues is connected to it with a line and each line labeled \(\rho_{Y_e, X_1}\) (with \(X_1\) replaced appropriately). To the right of the cues is a box labeled above in blue as &lsquo;subject response&rsquo; and inside as \(Y_s\). Each of the four cues is connected to it with a line and each line labeled \(\rho_{Y_s, X_1}\) (with \(X_1\) replaced appropriately). Below the cues is an equation \(r_a = \rho_{Y_e,Y_s}\) and below that in red the label &lsquo;achievement index&rsquo;. Lines connect the equation to both \(Y_e\) and \(Y_s\). Below this equation and label is another \(G= \rho_{\hat{Y}_e, \hat{Y}_s}\) and below that a label in red &lsquo;matching index&rsquo;. To the left a line goes to a box containing \(\hat{Y}_e\) and labeled in green &lsquo; predicted criterion value&rsquo;. A line from this box in turn goes to an equation \(R_e = \rho_{Y_e,\hat{Y}_e}\) which is labeled in green &lsquo;environmental predictability&rsquo;. A final line goes from this equation back to the original green box that was labeled &lsquo;criterion value&rsquo; To the right of &lsquo;matching index&rsquo; goes a line to a box containing \(\hat{Y}_{s}\) and labeled in blue &lsquo; predicted subject responses&rsquo;. A line from this box in turn goes to an equation \(R_s = \rho_{Y_s,\hat{Y}_s}\) which is labeled in blue &lsquo;response linearity&rsquo;. A final line goes from this equation back to the original blue box that was labeled &lsquo;subject response&rsquo;. In the lower left is the equation \[Y_e = \sum^{n}_{i=1} \beta_{e,j}X_j + \epsilon_e\] Under the right hand part of the equation but not including \({}+\epsilon_e\) is a horizontal brace labeled \(\hat{Y}_e\) In the lower right is the equation \[Y_s = \sum^{n}_{i=1} \beta_{s,j}X_j + \epsilon_s\] Under the right hand part of the equation but not including \({}+\epsilon_e\) is a horizontal brace labeled \(\hat{Y}_s\) Figure 3 description A diagram of four targets in a two by two layout. Each of the four targets consists of five concentric rings with the innermost ring colored gray. From innermost to outermost the rings are numbered 0 through 4. The top left target is labeled &lsquo;(low bias &amp; low variance)&rsquo;. A cluster of black dots is in ring 0. The top right target is labeled &lsquo;(low bias &amp; high variance)&rsquo;. Black dots are scattered at all angles in rings 0 through 2. The bottom left target is labeled &lsquo;(high bias &amp; low variance)&rsquo;. Black dots are clustered in the upper right quadrant in rings 2 and 3. The bottom right target is labeled &lsquo;(high bias &amp; high variance)&rsquo;. Black dots are mostly in the bottom right quadrant though some in the top right quadrant in all rings.
