Olivia has worked as both a Writer and Associate Editor for Simply Psychology since 2020, primarily in the areas of Neuroscience, Mental Health, and Psychological Theories. Her articles have been internationally cited in academic papers, her most cited being Bronfenbrenner&#8217;s Ecological Systems Theory . Aside from writing, Olivia is an autistic PhD researcher at the University of Birmingham. Her PhD is focused on exploring Autistic Camouflaging in Higher Educational Settings.
