Prevalence of ASD Prevalence data for ASD comes from the CDC&rsquo;s Autism and Developmental Disabilities Monitoring (ADDM) Network&nbsp; . The table below shows data from the most recent ADDM Network prevalence estimates&nbsp; (published April 2025). Data were collected from health and special education records of 8-year-old children living in 16 areas of the United States during 2022. Across the CDC surveillance sites, an average of 1 in every 31 (3.2%) 8-year-old children were estimated to have ASD in 2022. ASD is 3.4&nbsp;times as prevalent among boys (4.9%) as among girls (1.4%). ASD is reported to occur in all racial and ethnic groups. Measurement Caveats ASD prevalence estimates and median age of identification vary widely across sites and states. The final numbers only represent averages from across the states included in the ADDM Network. These data may not be interpreted as applying to the overall U.S. prevalence rate for ASD. The data presented here were collected only for 8-year-olds. These data may not be extrapolated and applied for other age cohorts.
