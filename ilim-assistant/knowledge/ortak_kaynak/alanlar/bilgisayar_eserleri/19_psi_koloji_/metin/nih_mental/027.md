The majority of the administrative staff are located in the Neuroscience Center Building in Rockville, Maryland. Most of the scientific and clinical research teams are based in Buildings 10, 31, and 35, located on the main NIH Campus in Bethesda, Maryland. Take the NIH Virtual Tour&nbsp; to explore the Bethesda campus to learn more about how NIH turns discovery into health. Directions to the Neuroscience Center are provided below. For information on traveling to the NIH campus, finding specific NIH buildings, and getting off-campus maps, check out NIH's Visitor Information&nbsp; . Via Metrorail&nbsp; Metrorail service is available from Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport and from Union Station (railway). Take Metro's Red Line to the Medical Center Metro station. From the station, NIH employees can take the "Executive Plaza" (also known as the orange route) shuttle bus, which drops you off in front of building 6001. You can also exit the Red Line at the North Bethesda Station and board a Ride-On bus (routes 5, 26, or 81) for service along Executive Boulevard. For more information, please see http://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/dot-transit/index.html&nbsp; .
