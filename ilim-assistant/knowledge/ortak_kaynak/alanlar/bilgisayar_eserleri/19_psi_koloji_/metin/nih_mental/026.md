To fulfill this vital public health mission, the Institute supports biomedical and behavioral research, health services research, research training, and health information dissemination with respect to the causes, diagnosis, treatment, management, and prevention of mental illnesses. This approach aims to turn scientific breakthroughs into real-world benefits for all people impacted by mental illnesses. NIMH History In 1946, President Harry S. Truman signed the National Mental Health Act. This landmark piece of legislation, with its broad purpose of improving "the mental health of the people of the United States," enabled the founding, three years later, of NIMH. Since its inception, NIMH has played a pivotal role in advancing the understanding, treatment, and prevention of mental illnesses.
