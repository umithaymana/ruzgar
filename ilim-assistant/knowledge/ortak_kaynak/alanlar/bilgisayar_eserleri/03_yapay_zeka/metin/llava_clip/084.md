For LLaVA model training, we consider a two-stage instruction-tuning procedure. Stage 1: Pre-training for Feature Alignment. To strike a balance between concept coverage and training efficiency, we filter CC3M to 595K image-text pairs. Please see Appendix for details of the filtering process.
