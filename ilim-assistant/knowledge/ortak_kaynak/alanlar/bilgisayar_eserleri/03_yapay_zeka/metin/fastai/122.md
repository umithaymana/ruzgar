Wu, Simultaneous robot-world and hand-eye calibration using dual-quaternions and kronecker product [168] The following picture describes the Robot-World/Hand-Eye calibration problem where the transformations between a robot and a world frame and between a robot gripper ("hand") and a camera ("eye") mounted at the robot end-effector have to be estimated.
