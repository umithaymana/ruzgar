Velocity and Acceleration of the Medium As seen in Example 16.4 , the wave speed is constant and represents the speed of the wave as it propagates through the medium, not the speed of the particles that make up the medium. The particles of the medium oscillate around an equilibrium position as the wave propagates through the medium. In the case of the transverse wave propagating in the x -direction, the particles oscillate up and down in the y -direction, perpendicular to the motion of the wave. The velocity of the particles of the medium is not constant, which means there is an acceleration.
