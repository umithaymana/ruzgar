Bulk Stress, Strain, and Modulus When you dive into water, you feel a force pressing on every part of your body from all directions. What you are experiencing then is bulk stress, or in other words, pressure . Bulk stress always tends to decrease the volume enclosed by the surface of a submerged object. The forces of this “squeezing” are always perpendicular to the submerged surface Figure 12.22 . The effect of these forces is to decrease the volume of the submerged object by an amount Δ V Δ V compared with the volume V 0 V 0 of the object in the absence of bulk stress.
