Vectors are usually described in terms of their components in a coordinate system . Even in everyday life we naturally invoke the concept of vector components in a rectangular coordinate system. For example, if you ask someone for directions to a particular location, you will more likely be told to go 40 km east and 30 km north than 50 km in the direction 37 ° 37 ° north of east.
