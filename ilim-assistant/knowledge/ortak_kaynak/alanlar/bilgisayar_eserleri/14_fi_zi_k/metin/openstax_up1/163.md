Significance Suppose that the mechanical system considered in this example is attached to a ceiling inside an elevator going up. As long as the elevator moves up at a constant speed, the result stays the same because the weight w w does not change. If the elevator moves up with acceleration, the critical mass is smaller because the weight of M + m M + m becomes larger by an apparent weight due to the acceleration of the elevator.
