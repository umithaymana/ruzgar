Problem-Solving Strategy Rocket Propulsion In rocket problems, the most common questions are finding the change of velocity due to burning some amount of fuel for some amount of time; or to determine the acceleration that results from burning fuel. To determine the change of velocity, use the rocket equation Equation 9.38 . To determine the acceleration, determine the force by using the impulse-momentum theorem, using the rocket equation to determine the change of velocity.
