Cylindrical Solenoid Consider a long, cylindrical solenoid with length l , cross-sectional area A , and N turns of wire. We assume that the length of the solenoid is so much larger than its diameter that we can take the magnetic field to be B = μ 0 n I B = μ 0 n I throughout the interior of the solenoid, that is, we ignore end effects in the solenoid. Notice that the self-inductance of a long solenoid depends only on its physical properties (such as the number of turns of wire per unit length and the volume), and not on the magnetic field or the current.
