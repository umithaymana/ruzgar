(credit: modification of work by Bert Hickman) Chapter Outline 8.1 Capacitors and Capacitance 8.2 Capacitors in Series and in Parallel 8.3 Energy Stored in a Capacitor 8.4 Capacitor with a Dielectric 8.5 Molecular Model of a Dielectric Introduction Capacitors are important components of electrical circuits in many electronic devices, including pacemakers, cell phones, and computers. In this chapter, we study their properties, and, over the next few chapters, we examine their function in combination with other circuit elements. By themselves, capacitors are often used to store electrical energy and release it when needed; with other circuit components, capacitors often act as part of a filter that allows some electrical signals to pass while blocking others. You can see why capacitors are considered one of the fundamental components of electrical circuits.
