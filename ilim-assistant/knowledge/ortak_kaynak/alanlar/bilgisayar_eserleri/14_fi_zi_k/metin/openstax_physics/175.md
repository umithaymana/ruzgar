Derivation of Kepler’s Third Law for Circular Orbits We shall derive Kepler’s third law, starting with Newton’s laws of motion and his universal law of gravitation. The point is to demonstrate that the force of gravity is the cause for Kepler’s laws (although we will only derive the third one). Let us consider a circular orbit of a small mass m m around a large mass M M , satisfying the two conditions stated at the beginning of this section.
