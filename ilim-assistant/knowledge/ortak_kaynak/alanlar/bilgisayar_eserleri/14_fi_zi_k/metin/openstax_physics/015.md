Example 3.1 Delivering Flyers Jill sets out from her home to deliver flyers for her yard sale, traveling due east along her street lined with houses. At 0.5 0.5 km and 9 minutes later she runs out of flyers and has to retrace her steps back to her house to get more. This takes an additional 9 minutes.
