Having computed the eccentric anomaly E , the next step is to calculate the true anomaly θ . Note that Cartesian position coordinates with reference to the center of ellipse are ( a cos E , b sin E ). With reference to the Sun (with coordinates ( c , 0) = ( ae , 0)), r = ( a cos E − ae , b sin E ). True anomaly would be arctan( r y / r x ), magnitude of r would be √ r · r .
