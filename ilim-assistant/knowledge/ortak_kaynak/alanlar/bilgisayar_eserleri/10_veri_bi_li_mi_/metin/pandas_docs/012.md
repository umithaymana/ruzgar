Before reaching for apply , look for a vectorized alternative: a DataFrame or Series method, an arithmetic or comparison operation (see Flexible binary operations ), or an accessor method such as the vectorized string methods under .str or the datetime properties under .dt . Reserve apply for logic with no vectorized equivalent, such as a function from a third-party library that operates on one row or column at a time. The apply() method will also dispatch on a string method name.
