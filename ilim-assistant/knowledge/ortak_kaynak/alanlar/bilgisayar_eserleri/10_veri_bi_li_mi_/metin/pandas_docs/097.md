The 'numexpr' engine is the more performant engine that can yield performance improvements compared to standard Python syntax for large DataFrame . You will only see the performance benefits of using the numexpr engine with pandas.eval() if your DataFrame has more than approximately 100,000 rows. Even then, the benefit depends on expression complexity: the more operations in the expression, the greater the potential speedup. For simple expressions (e.g., df[&quot;a&quot;] + df[&quot;b&quot;] ), standard Python/NumPy operations will be faster regardless of DataFrame size because eval() has parsing and compilation overhead that outweighs any benefit from numexpr .
