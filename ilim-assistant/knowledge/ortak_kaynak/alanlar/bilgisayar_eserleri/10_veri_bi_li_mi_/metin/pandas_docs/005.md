DataFrame # DataFrame is a 2-dimensional labeled data structure with columns of potentially different types. You can think of it like a spreadsheet or SQL table, or a dict of Series objects. It is generally the most commonly used pandas object. Like Series, DataFrame accepts many different kinds of input: Dict of 1D ndarrays, lists, dicts, or Series 2-D numpy.ndarray Structured or record ndarray A Series Another DataFrame Along with the data, you can optionally pass index (row labels) and columns (column labels) arguments. If you pass an index and / or columns, you are guaranteeing the index and / or columns of the resulting DataFrame. Thus, a dict of Series plus a specific index will discard all data not matching up to the passed index. If axis labels are not passed, they will be constructed from the input data based on common sense rules.
