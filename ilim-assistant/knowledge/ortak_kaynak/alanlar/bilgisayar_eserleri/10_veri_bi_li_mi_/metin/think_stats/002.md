x Preface
I assume that the reader knows basic mathematics, including logarithms, for
example, and summations. I refer to calculus concepts in a few places, but
you don’t have to do any calculus.
If you have never studied statistics, I think this book is a good place to start.
And if you have taken a traditional statistics class, I hope this book will help
repair the damage.
—
Allen B. Downey is a Professor of Computer Science at the Franklin W. Olin
College of Engineering in Needham, MA.
