All models, including an optional base, must have matching module configurations, tokenizer vocabularies, state keys, and tied weights. Models should be fine-tunes of a common ancestor. The chosen method applies to every floating-point tensor, including projection and classification heads. Compatibility is checked before merging. For example, different pooling modes, transformer configurations, or token-to-ID mappings are rejected even if weight shapes match. Saved transformer metadata and dtype declarations may differ, and model-level prompts may differ because the first model supplies the output’s prompts, but successful validation does not guarantee improved task performance. All input weights and the merged result are held in memory. The inputs are left unchanged. Configuration, prompts, and processors are inherited from the first model, and a fresh model card is generated when saving.
