A CrossEncoder does not produce sentence embeddings. Instead, it processes both sentences jointly through the transformer and outputs a score (regression) or class probabilities (classification). This makes it more accurate for pairwise tasks like reranking or semantic textual similarity, but it cannot pre-compute embeddings for individual sentences.
