15.2.2 Multiclass SVMs
SVMs are inherently two-class classiﬁers. The traditional way to do mul-
ticlass classiﬁcation with SVMs is to use one of the methods d iscussed in
Section 14.5 (page 306). In particular, the most common technique in prac-
tice has been to build|C| one-versus-rest classiﬁers (commonly referred to as
“one-versus-all” or OVA classiﬁcation), and to choose the c lass which classi-
ﬁes the test datum with greatest margin. Another strategy is to build a set
of one-versus-one classiﬁers, and to choose the class that i s selected by the
most classiﬁers. While this involves building |C|(|C|− 1)/2 classiﬁers, the
time for training classiﬁers may actually decrease, since t he training data set
for each classiﬁer is much smaller.
However, these are not very elegant approaches to solving multiclass prob-
lems. A better alternative is provided by the construction of multiclass SVMs,
