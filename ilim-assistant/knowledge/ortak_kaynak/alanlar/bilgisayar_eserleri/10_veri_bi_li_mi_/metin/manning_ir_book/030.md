5.4 References and further reading
Heaps’ law was discovered byHeaps (1978). See also Baeza-Yates and Ribeiro-
Neto (1999). A detailed study of vocabulary growth in large collection s is
(Williams and Zobel 2005). Zipf’s law is due to Zipf (1949). Witten and Bell
(1990) investigate the quality of the ﬁt obtained by the law. Other term distri-
bution models, including K mixture and two-poisson model, a re discussed
by Manning and Schütze (1999, Chapter 15). Carmel et al. (2001), Büttcher
and Clarke (2006), Blanco and Barreiro (2007), and Ntoulas and Cho (2007)
show that lossy compression can achieve good compression wi th no or no
signiﬁcant decrease in retrieval effectiveness.
Dictionary compression is covered in detail by Witten et al. (1999, Chap-
ter 4), which is recommended as additional reading.
