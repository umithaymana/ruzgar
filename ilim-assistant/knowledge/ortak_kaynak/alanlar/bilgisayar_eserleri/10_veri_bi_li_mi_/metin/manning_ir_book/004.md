We now cannot build a term-document matrix in a naive way. A 50 0K×
1M matrix has half-a-trillion 0’s and 1’s – too many to ﬁt in a c omputer’s
memory. But the crucial observation is that the matrix is ext remely sparse,
that is, it has few non-zero entries. Because each document i s 1000 words
long, the matrix has no more than one billion 1’s, so a minimum of 99.8% of
the cells are zero. A much better representation is to record only the things
that do occur, that is, the 1 positions.
