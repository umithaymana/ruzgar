Paths, NixOS options, environment variables Constant paths should be marked with Pa , NixOS options with Va , and environment variables with Ev . Generated paths, e.g. result/bin/run-hostname-vm (where hostname is a variable or arguments) should be marked as Ql inline literals with their variable components marked appropriately. When hostname refers to an argument, it becomes .Ql result/bin/run- Ns Ar hostname Ns -vm When hostname refers to a variable, it becomes .Ql result/bin/run- Ns Va hostname Ns -vm Code examples and other commands In free text names and complete invocations of other commands (e.g. ssh or tar -xvf src.tar ) should be marked with Ic , fragments of command lines should be marked with Ql .
