The pipe-operators experimental feature adds &lt;| and |&gt; operators to the Nix language. a |&gt; b is equivalent to the function application b a , and a &lt;| b is equivalent to the function application a b . For example: nix-repl&gt; 1 |&gt; builtins.add 2 |&gt; builtins.mul 3 9 nix-repl&gt; builtins.add 1 &lt;| builtins.mul 2 &lt;| 3 7 &lt;| and |&gt; are right and left associative, respectively, and have lower precedence than any other operator. These properties may change in future releases.
