Therefore, the outer recording zones are faster (higher bandwidth) than the inner zones by the ratio of outer to inner track diameter. The difference in bandwidth can be 2:1, and is often available in the HDD detailed specifications or drive manual. For HDDs when metaslab_lba_weighting_enabled is true, write allocation preference is given to the metaslabs representing the outer recording zones.
