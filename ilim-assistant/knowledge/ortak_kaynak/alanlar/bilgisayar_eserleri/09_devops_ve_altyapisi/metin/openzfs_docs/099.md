Upgrade or downgrade the Areca driver if something like RIP: 0010:[&lt;ffffffff8101b316&gt;] &#160; [&lt;ffffffff8101b316&gt;] native_read_tsc+0x6/0x20 appears anywhere in kernel log. MPT2SAS  Most problem reports for this tutorial involve mpt2sas hardware that does slow asynchronous drive initialization, like some IBM M1015 or OEM-branded cards that have been flashed to the reference LSI firmware. The basic problem is that disks on these controllers are not visible to the Linux kernel until after the regular system is started, and ZoL does not hotplug pool members. See https://github.com/openzfs/zfs/issues/330 . Most LSI cards are perfectly compatible with ZoL. If your card has this glitch, try setting ZFS_INITRD_PRE_MOUNTROOT_SLEEP=X in /etc/default/zfs . The system will wait X seconds for all drives to appear before importing the pool.
