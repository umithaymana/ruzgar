This is phrased as “insensitivity” because larger values result in fewer detections. Smaller values will result in more aggressive sitting out of disks that may have problems, but may significantly increase the rate of spurious sit-outs. To provide a more technical definition of this parameter, this is the multiple of the inter-quartile range (IQR) that is being used in a Tukey’s Fence detection algorithm.
