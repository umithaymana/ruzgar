This article addresses the most common scalability concerns around a pull-based monitoring approach. With Prometheus and other pull-based systems being used successfully in very large environments and the pull aspect not posing a bottleneck in reality, the result should be clear: the “pull doesn't scale” argument is not a real concern. We hope that future debates will focus on aspects that matter more than this red herring.
