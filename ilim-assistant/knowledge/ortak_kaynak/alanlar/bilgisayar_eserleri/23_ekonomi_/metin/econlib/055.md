market is any structure that allows buyers and sellers to exchange goods, services and information . The exchange of goods or services, either with money or without it , is a transaction . [ 1 ] In free market economies, they are the process by which prices and allocations of goods and services are established. Markets facilitate trade and enable the distribution and allocation of resources in a society by allowing tradeable items to be evaluated and priced .
