Deprecated, do not use. tonemapping_knee_adaptation Controls how strongly the knee point adapts to the source scene average brightness, relative to the target average in PQ space. Defaults to 0.8 . tonemapping_knee_default Sets the fallback knee point used when source scene average brightness metadata is unavailable. Defaults to 0.2 . tonemapping_spline_contrast Controls the contrast of the spline curve. Higher values produce a steeper curve, preserving midtone contrast at the cost of shadow and highlight detail. Defaults to 0.5 . tonemapping_linear_knee Sets the knee point for the legacy mobius and gamma curves, which operate on linear light. Defaults to 0.3 . tonemapping_exposure Sets the linear exposure or gain applied by the linear tone-mapping method. Note that this figure is squared when combined with peak_detect . contrast_recovery Contrast recovery strength. If set to a value above 0.0 , the source image will be divided into high-frequency and low-frequency components, and a portion of the high-frequency image is added back onto the tone-mapped output. It&rsquo;s recommended to always leave this on, since not doing so may result in visible banding in the output, even if the debanding filter is enabled. If maximum performance is needed, use ordered_fixed instead of disabling dithering. dithering Dithering method to use. Accepts the following values: &lsquo; none &rsquo; Disables dithering completely. May result in visible banding. &lsquo; blue &rsquo; Dither with pseudo-blue noise. This is the default. &lsquo; ordered &rsquo; Tunable ordered dither pattern. &lsquo; ordered_fixed &rsquo; Faster ordered dither with a fixed size of 6 . Texture-less. &lsquo; white &rsquo; Dither with white noise. Texture-less. dither_lut_size Dither LUT size, as log base2 between 1 and 8 . Defaults to 6 , corresponding to a LUT size of 64x64 . dither_temporal Enables temporal dithering.
