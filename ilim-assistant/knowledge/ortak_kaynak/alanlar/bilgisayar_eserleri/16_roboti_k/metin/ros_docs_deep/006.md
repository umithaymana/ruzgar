Each official Working Group has a charter, ratified by the ROS PMC, that defines its purpose, tasks, expected outputs, meeting frequency, and completion date. The ratified charters are published in the `OSRA Policies and Procedures repository `__. Working Group meetings are held in the open, and anyone is welcome to participate. Meetings are announced on `Open Robotics Discourse `__ and listed on the `official OSRA Google calendar `__. To receive meeting invites automatically, join the Google Group associated with the Working Group. The Chair of each Working Group reports on its activities at the ROS PMC meetings. The following Working Groups are currently chartered by the ROS PMC: .. list-table:: :header-rows: 1 * - Working Group - Chair - Charter - Resources * - Accelerated Memory Transport - `Alejandro Hernández Cordero `_ - `Charter `__ (expires 2027-10-07) - * `Meeting agendas and minutes `__ * `Kickoff announcement on Discourse `__ * Zulip channel: `#Accelerated Memory Transport Working Group `__ * - Client Libraries - `Alberto Soragna `_ - `Charter `__ (expires 2027-09-04) - * Meeting invite group: `ros-2-client-library-wg `__ * `Meeting agendas and minutes `__ * Discourse tag: `wg-client-libraries `__ * - ROSGraph - `Emerson Knapp `_ - `Charter `__ (expires 2026-11-03) - * Meeting invite group: `rosgraph-wg `__ * `Meeting agendas and minutes `__ * `Kickoff announcement on Discourse `__ * `Project board `__ If you would like to join an existing Working Group, join its meeting invite group or contact its Chair directly. If you would like to propose a new Working Group, draft a charter using the `Working Group charter template `__, open a pull request against the OSRA Policies and Procedures repository, and ask one of the :ref:`ROS PMC constituents ` to bring it to a ROS PMC meeting for ratification. Note that the Chair of an official Working Group must be a ROS PMC Member. Community groups that are organized by and for the ROS community, but are not chartered by the ROS PMC, are not listed here. Their meetings can be found on the `Discourse working-group tag `__ and on the unofficial ROS community calendar below.
