View on GitHub Documentation Commits 46,000+ Contributors 350 Open-source projects 47 Building since 2012 About OWASP Bug Logging Tool Introduction OWASP BLT (Bug Logging Tool) lets everyone who uses the internet help make it better. Spot a broken link, a typo, a 500 error or a serious vulnerability on any website, app or repository, and log it on BLT. Public issues are verified by the community, security issues are disclosed privately, and the organizations involved are held to account until they're fixed. What started in 2012 as an idea to make a game out of bug finding has grown into a family of 47 open-source projects , built by 350 contributors in more than 46,000 commits . All of it is free and open source at git.owaspblt.org/BLT . Visit owaspblt.org Browse the source What BLT Does 🐛 Report anything. Log any bug, with screenshots, from the web, the mobile app, the browser extension, the terminal or an AI agent. 🔒 Disclose responsibly. Send sensitive findings through BLT Zero 's encrypted, zero-log channel. Accepted vulnerabilities can get an ID in the BLT-YYYY-NNNN registry . 🏆 Reward the finders. Earn points, climb leaderboards, win bug hunts and hackathons , and get recognized for every fix. 🎓 Grow security talent. Take free courses and browser labs at BLT University , make a guided first contribution, and join a mentored Google Summer of Code project. How It Works For bug hunters Spot something wrong on any site, app or repository. Log it on owaspblt.org with a description and a screenshot. Security issues go privately. Earn points, bounties and recognition as it gets verified and fixed. For organizations Claim your domain and see every report about it in one place. Launch a bug hunt with a prize pool, or a hackathon for your contributors. Fix what's reported and give your users a bug-free experience. The BLT Family Every BLT project lives in one open monorepo at git.owaspblt.org/BLT , and most run live on the edge. Browse the project ideas , start contributing early, and find everything else on gsoc.owaspblt.org . Frequently Asked Questions What is OWASP BLT? A free, open-source set of tools for reporting bugs and vulnerabilities on any website, app or repository, rewarding the people who find them, and teaching security through real contributions. Who can use it? Anyone. You don't need a security background to report a broken page, and experienced researchers get private disclosure, bug hunts and bounties. How do I report a security vulnerability? Never in a public issue. Use BLT Zero for encrypted reports about any site. For vulnerabilities in BLT itself, follow the Security Policy . Can my organization run a bug hunt? Yes. Launch a bug hunt with a prize pool on owaspblt.org , or a contributor hackathon on BLT Hackathons . Where is the code? Is GitHub still used? All BLT code, issues and pull requests are at git.owaspblt.org/BLT . github.com/OWASP/BLT is a read-only mirror kept as a backup. Is it free? Yes. Each project keeps its own open-source license; most are AGPL-3.0.
