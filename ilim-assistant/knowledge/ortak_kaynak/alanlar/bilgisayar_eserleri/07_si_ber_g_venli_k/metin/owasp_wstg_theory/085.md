Compliance for AI Agent Payments Cheat Sheet &para; Introduction &para; AI agents are initiating regulated financial transactions in production. Mastercard Agent Pay, Visa Intelligent Commerce, and Google A2A payments are live. Using an AI agent does not remove applicable Anti-Money Laundering (AML) or sanctions obligations. Determine the requirements for the institution, transaction, customer relationship, and jurisdiction before implementing the controls below. This cheat sheet provides practical controls for fintechs, banks, and payment processors when autonomous AI agents -- rather than human users in browser sessions -- initiate or facilitate regulated payments. It covers agent identity verification, entity screening, audit trail requirements, and fail-closed enforcement. Use the MCP authentication and authorization guidance when exposing screening tools through MCP. Message-level signing is an optional additional control , not a core MCP requirement. The signed-audit and receipt design in Sections 4 and 8-10 is one option for systems that need verification beyond a transport connection. Regulatory Context &para; The applicable obligations depend on the institution's regulated role, the transaction, and the jurisdictions involved. Have compliance owners identify the requirements the payment service must enforce: Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) : US BSA regulations establish recordkeeping, reporting, and other requirements for covered financial institutions and businesses. Determine which requirements apply to the service's activities. Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) Sanctions : Comply with applicable sanctions prohibitions and blocking requirements. Screening supports those controls, but OFAC does not impose a general requirement to scan names or use screening software . Complete the necessary analysis before concluding a transaction. Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) : Apply Customer Identification Program (CIP) and Customer Due Diligence (CDD) requirements to the relevant customers and beneficial owners under the institution's applicable rules and exceptions. The CDD rule covers specified types of financial institutions ; authenticating software does not identify the legal customer.
