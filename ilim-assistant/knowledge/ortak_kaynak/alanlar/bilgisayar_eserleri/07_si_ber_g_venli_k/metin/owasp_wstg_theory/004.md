Join Our Community → Our Story 250+ Global Chapters 100K+ Community Members 275+ Active Projects 25+ Years of Impact The OWASP Foundation The Open Worldwide Application Security Project (OWASP) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit foundation that works to improve the security of software. The OWASP project launched on December 1st, 2001, becoming incorporated as the OWASP Foundation, Inc., a United States nonprofit on April 21, 2004. Our programming includes Open Source Projects Community-led open source projects including code, documentation, and standards Local Chapters Over 250+ local chapters worldwide Members Tens of thousands of members Conferences &amp; Training Industry-leading educational and training conferences We are an open community dedicated to enabling organizations to conceive, develop, acquire, operate, and maintain applications that can be trusted. All of our projects, tools, documents, forums, and chapters are free and open to anyone interested in improving application security. Our Journey A quarter century of dedication to making software security visible, so people and organizations can make informed decisions.
