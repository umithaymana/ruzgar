OWASP Threat Dragon | OWASP Foundation OWASP Foundation Sign in Donate OWASP Threat Dragon OWASP Threat Dragon is a free, open-source, cross-platform threat modeling application used to draw threat modeling diagrams and to list threats for elements in the diagram. Threat Dragon is designed to be accessible, with an emphasis on flexibility and simplicity. It supports multiple threat modeling frameworks including STRIDE, CIA, LINDDUN, and more. You can run Threat Dragon as a standalone desktop application or a fully featured, configurable, self-hosted web application.
