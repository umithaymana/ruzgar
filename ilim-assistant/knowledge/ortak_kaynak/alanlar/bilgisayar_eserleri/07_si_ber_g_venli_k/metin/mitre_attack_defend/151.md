AN2223 Embedded ICS DET0799 Near-real-time connection monitoring identifies an embedded field device or gateway initiating outbound connections on standard application-layer protocol ports to destinations outside the expected Industrial Control System (ICS) communication baseline. OT operational-fidelity gate: Alert only when the asset's documented role does not permit the connection and the destination/proxy/service is outside the approved communications profile, with process/service identity used as corroboration where available. Suppress approved vendor support, update repositories, remote operations, failover paths, and documented temporary connectivity. This analytic is implementable from session/flow metadata (endpoints, ports, protocol/service, timing, bytes); it does not require or claim packet-payload visibility.
