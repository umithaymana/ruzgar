Expected The threat event or TTP has been seen by the organization’s peers or partners. Anticipated The threat event or TTP has been reported by a trusted source. Predicted The threat event or TTP has been predicted by a trusted source. Possible The threat event or TTP has been described by a somewhat credible source. N/A The threat event or TTP is not currently applicable. For example, a threat event or TTP could assume specific technologies, architectures, or processes that are not present in the organization, mission/business process, EA segment, or information system; or predisposing conditions that are not present (e.g., location in a flood plain). Alternately, if the organization is using detailed or specific threat information, a threat event or TTP could be deemed inapplicable because information indicates that no adversary is expected to initiate the threat event or use the TTP.
