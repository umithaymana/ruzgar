Entity IDs, unique IDs, and long-term statistics are unchanged. Who is affected: only devices that have ever recorded reverse flow (backflow). The value steps up once, by the device’s lifetime backflow, at the first update after upgrading. Because these sensors are total_increasing , Home Assistant records that step as consumption, so the Energy dashboard and statistics show a one-time spike. Devices with no recorded backflow see no change. How to recover: adjust the spike away with Adjust a statistic in Settings &gt; Tools &gt; Statistics , or ignore it. Nothing needs to be reconfigured. Why: the old value silently hid reverse flow and was not the monotonically increasing total the state class promises. The new Water meter negative volume/duration sensors expose the reverse reading separately. On firmware that only reports a single (differential) meter, Water meter volume/duration keep reporting that differential and the new negative sensors are unavailable.
