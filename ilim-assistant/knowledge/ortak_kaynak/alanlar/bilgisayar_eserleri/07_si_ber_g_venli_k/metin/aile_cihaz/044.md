Yes, Connect ZBT-2 is being used by many people with Zigbee2MQTT, and they are seeing great results. There is documentation to help support migrating to a new adapter, like Connect ZBT-2, on Zigbee2MQTT . Why do I have to choose between Zigbee and Thread? Why not both? Connect ZBT-2 cannot do both Zigbee and Thread simultaneously. This technology is often called “multiprotocol” or “MultiPAN”. Though it is theoretically possible with the hardware within Connect ZBT-2, in our experience, this functionality doesn’t work well, and we don’t plan to implement it. We previously thoroughly tested multiprotocol with our Connect ZBT-1 adapter and found its operation to be inconsistent, often causing device stability issues. We do not believe multiprotocol is suitable for operation in the home, and strongly recommend dedicating a device to each protocol. Can I migrate from my old adapter? Yes, we’ve put together easy migration wizards for both Zigbee and Thread, which help migrate the vast majority of adapters. Some adapters require extra steps before migrating to Connect ZBT-2 (for example, Conbee sticks require a specific firmware to facilitate the migration). Please visit our documentation for specific instructions on how to migrate your adapter . Why are you not using Power-over-Ethernet (PoE)? Though PoE is a cool technology, we have focused this product on simplicity and peak performance. PoE has become easier to use and offers performance levels that make its convenience an appealing trade-off for many users, but it didn’t fit Connect ZBT-2’s design goals.
