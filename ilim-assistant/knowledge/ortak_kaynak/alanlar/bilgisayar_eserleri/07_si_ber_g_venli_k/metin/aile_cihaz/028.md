Batteryless switches with Zigbee Green Power Zigbee Home Automation now supports Zigbee Green Power. Green Power devices A device is a model representing a physical or logical unit that contains entities. run without a battery: a wall switch powered by the energy of your press, or a sensor that lives off the light in the room. Friends of Hue switches are the best-known example. Until now, Home Assistant passed them by. They work differently from other Zigbee devices, and the interface follows along. A Green Power device lists its Source ID with its network details, and the options that don’t apply to it, like reconfiguring it or managing its clusters, are left out. In the network visualization it shows up without a line to a parent, because a Green Power device doesn’t have one. Thanks, @puddly !
