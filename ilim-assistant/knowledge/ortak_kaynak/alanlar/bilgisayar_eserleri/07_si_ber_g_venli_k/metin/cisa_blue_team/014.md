Achieve Essential Cyber Hygiene Almost all successful cyber attacks exploit &ldquo;poor cyber hygiene&rdquo; like unpatched software, poor configuration management, and outdated solutions. The CIS Controls include foundational security measures that you can use to achieve essential hygiene and protect yourself against a cyber attack.
