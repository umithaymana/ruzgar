An Information Sharing and Analysis Center (ISAC) collects, analyzes, and disseminates actionable threat information to members and provides them with tools to mitigate risks and improve resiliency. Join MS-ISAC CIS is home to the MS-ISAC The scale and impact of cyber-attacks continue to escalate. While many public sector organizations are challenged to keep pace, there is help available. The Center for Internet Security&reg; (CIS&reg;) is home to the Multi-State Information Sharing and Analysis Center&reg; (MS-ISAC&reg;). It provides a variety of services that augment and enhance members' cybersecurity teams. As a member you'll get access to a community that will help you stay up to date on cyber threats facing your industry. You will also receive tools, tips, and tricks for defending against these cyber threats. Here are just some of the benefits of joining: Always-on protection: A 24x7x365 U.S.-based SOC delivers threat intelligence, detection, and response support whenever you need it Skills that stick: Hands-on tabletop exercises and regular webinars keep your team prepared Know where you stand: The MS-ISAC Assessment Tool tracks your cybersecurity posture over time Tools to act: CIS SecureSuite gives you integrated resources to put best practices into practice View all membership benefits The mission of the Multi-State Information Sharing &amp; Analysis Center (MS-ISAC) is to improve the overall cybersecurity posture of U.S. State, Local, Tribal, and Territorial (SLTT) government organizations through coordination, collaboration, cooperation, and increased communication. Membership is open to all U.S. SLTT government organizations.
