They are mapped to and referenced by multiple legal, regulatory, and policy frameworks. Download CIS Controls v8 Want to learn more about the CIS Controls? Check out our video below. Watch video CIS Controls v8 was enhanced to keep up with modern systems and software. Movement to cloud-based computing, virtualization, mobility, outsourcing, work from home, and changing attacker tactics prompted the update and supports an enterprise&rsquo;s security as they move to both fully cloud and hybrid environments. Our design principles included: Offense Informs Defense CIS Controls are selected, dropped, and prioritized based on data and on specific knowledge of attacker behavior and how to stop it. Focus Help defenders identify the most critical things they need to do to stop the most important attacks. Avoid being tempted to solve every security problem &mdash; avoid adding &ldquo;good things to do&rdquo; or &ldquo;things you could do.&rdquo; Feasible All individual CIS Safeguards must be specific and practical to implement. Measurable All CIS Controls, especially for Implementation Group 1, must be measurable. Simplify or remove ambiguous language to avoid inconsistent interpretation. Some Safeguards may have a threshold. Align Create and demonstrate &ldquo;peaceful co-existence&rdquo; with other governance, regulatory, process management schemes, framework, and structures. Cooperate with and point to existing, independent standards and security recommendations where they exist (e.g., National Institute of Standards and Technology&reg; (NIST&reg;), Cloud Security Alliance (CSA), Software Assurance Forum for Excellence in Code (SAFECode), MITRE ATT&CK&reg;, and Open Web Application Security Project&reg; (OWASP&reg;)).
