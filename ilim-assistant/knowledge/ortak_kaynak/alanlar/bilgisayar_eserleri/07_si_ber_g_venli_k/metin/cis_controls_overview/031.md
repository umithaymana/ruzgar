Watch video CIS Controls v8 was enhanced to keep up with modern systems and software. Movement to cloud-based computing, virtualization, mobility, outsourcing, work from home, and changing attacker tactics prompted the update and supports an enterprise&rsquo;s security as they move to both fully cloud and hybrid environments. Our design principles included: Offense Informs Defense CIS Controls are selected, dropped, and prioritized based on data and on specific knowledge of attacker behavior and how to stop it. Focus Help defenders identify the most critical things they need to do to stop the most important attacks. Avoid being tempted to solve every security problem &mdash; avoid adding &ldquo;good things to do&rdquo; or &ldquo;things you could do.&rdquo; Feasible All individual CIS Safeguards must be specific and practical to implement. Measurable All CIS Controls, especially for Implementation Group 1, must be measurable. Simplify or remove ambiguous language to avoid inconsistent interpretation. Some Safeguards may have a threshold. Align Create and demonstrate &ldquo;peaceful co-existence&rdquo; with other governance, regulatory, process management schemes, framework, and structures. Cooperate with and point to existing, independent standards and security recommendations where they exist (e.g., National Institute of Standards and Technology&reg; (NIST&reg;), Cloud Security Alliance (CSA), Software Assurance Forum for Excellence in Code (SAFECode), MITRE ATT&amp;CK&reg;, and Open Web Application Security Project&reg; (OWASP&reg;)). Download CIS Controls v7.1 Controls v7.1 was enhanced to ensure that every CIS Control is clear, concise, and current. This version set the foundation for a much more straightforward and manageable way to implement, measure, and automate the Controls. Specifically, v7.1 addressed the growing need for assistance with prioritizing and phasing in the CIS Controls into an enterprise's cybersecurity program with the creation of Implementation Groups (IGs). As such, the following principles were used to drive the v7.1 update. Reassess the prioritization scheme for the CIS Controls down to the level of Sub-Controls (now referred to as CIS Safeguards beginning in v8) given the evolving threat landscape and resource constraints; Fix minor typos and errors; Enhance the clarity and readability of the CIS Controls and Sub-Controls; and Refrain from adding or subtracting from the technical content, or &ldquo;spirit,&rdquo; of a CIS Control or Sub-Control.
