Achieve Essential Cyber Hygiene Almost all successful cyber attacks exploit &ldquo;poor cyber hygiene&rdquo; like unpatched software, poor configuration management, and outdated solutions. The CIS Controls include foundational security measures that you can use to achieve essential hygiene and protect yourself against a cyber attack. Translate Information into Action Modern systems and software are dynamic in nature. By enacting the CIS Controls, you support your assets' evolving needs in a meaningful way and align your security efforts with your business goals.
