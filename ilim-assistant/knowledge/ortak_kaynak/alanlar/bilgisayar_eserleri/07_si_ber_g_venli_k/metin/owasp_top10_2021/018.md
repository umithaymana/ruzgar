About the OWASP Board | OWASP Foundation OWASP Foundation Sign in Donate Global Board About The OWASP Foundation Global Board sets strategic direction and governance for the Foundation. About the OWASP Board The OWASP Foundation Global Board comprises seven elected members who serve two-year terms. From August to November, membership votes to elect new leadership for the Foundation. Generally, our Board meets monthly, and meetings are open to the public. The Global Board sets the strategic direction of the Foundation, its policies, annual budget, and sets governance and leadership roles. Meetings follow the Roberts Rules of Order, 12th Edition, and all public meetings are recorded. The OWASP Operations Europe iVZW has its own Board pages .
