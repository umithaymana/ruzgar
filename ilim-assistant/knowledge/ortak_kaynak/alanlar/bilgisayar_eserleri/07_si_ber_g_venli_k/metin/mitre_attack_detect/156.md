AN2292 SaaS Enterprise DET0941 An analyst observes a user-originated instruction submitted to an AI application that attempts to override protected instructions or operational constraints. Within the same authenticated interaction, the AI system subsequently attempts a tool invocation, resource access, or workflow transition inconsistent with the authorized task or capability scope. The analyst correlates the originating instruction, subsequent operation, and authorization context to identify a potential successful instruction-boundary violation. AN2293 SaaS Enterprise DET0942 An analyst observes an AI application retrieve or ingest lower-trust content containing instructions that attempt to redirect the application's behavior. Within the same agent execution context, the AI system subsequently attempts a tool invocation, resource access, or workflow transition outside the actions authorized for the original task or retrieved resource. The analyst correlates content provenance, instruction evidence, subsequent operation, and authorization context to identify potential promotion of untrusted content into operational authority. AN2294 SaaS Enterprise DET0943 An analyst observes an autonomous defensive system ingest an adversary-controlled artifact, message, or other lower-trust content during a security workflow. Following ingestion, the system unexpectedly terminates or truncates analysis, suppresses a detection, modifies a security decision, or attempts to disable a defensive capability contrary to the established workflow.
