2.6 Complex number arithmetic
Since complex numbers are legitimate mathematical entitie s, just like scalar numbers, they
can be added, subtracted, multiplied, divided, squared, in verted, and such, just like any other
kind of number . Some scientiﬁc calculators are programmed t o directly perform these opera-
tions on two or more complex numbers, but these operations ca n also be done “by hand.” This
section will show you how the basic operations are performed . It is highly recommended that
you equip yourself with a scientiﬁc calculator capable of pe rforming arithmetic functions easily
on complex numbers. It will make your study of AC circuit much more pleasant than if you’re
forced to do all calculations the longer way .
