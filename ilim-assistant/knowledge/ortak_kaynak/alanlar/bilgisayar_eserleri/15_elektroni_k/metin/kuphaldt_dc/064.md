10.3 Mesh current method
The Mesh Current Method , also known as the Loop Current Method , is quite similar to the
Branch Current method in that it uses simultaneous equation s, Kirchhoff ’s Voltage Law , and
Ohm’s Law to determine unknown currents in a network. It diff ers from the Branch Current
method in that it does not use Kirchhoff ’s Current Law , and it is usually able to solve a circuit
with less unknown variables and less simultaneous equation s, which is especially nice if you’re
forced to solve without a calculator .
