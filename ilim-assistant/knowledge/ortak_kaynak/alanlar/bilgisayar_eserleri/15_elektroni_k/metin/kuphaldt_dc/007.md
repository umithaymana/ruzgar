1.5 Resistance
The circuit in the previous section is not a very practical on e. In fact, it can be quite dangerous
to build (directly connecting the poles of a voltage source t ogether with a single piece of wire).
The reason it is dangerous is because the magnitude of electr ic current may be very large

Usually , electric circuits are constructed in such a way as to make practical use of that released
energy , in as safe a manner as possible.

simplest form of electric lamp is a tiny metal ”ﬁlament” insi de of a clear glass bulb, which glows
