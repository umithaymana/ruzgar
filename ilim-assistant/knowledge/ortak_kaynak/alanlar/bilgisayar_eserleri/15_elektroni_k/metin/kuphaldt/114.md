3.10 Diode switching circuits
Diodes can perform switching and digital logic operations. Forward and reverse bias switch a
diode between the low and high impedance states, respective ly . Thus, it serves as a switch.

computers. It only ﬁnds limited application today . Sometim es it is convenient to fashion a
