In summary , a series circuit is deﬁned as having only one path for electrons to ﬂow . From
this deﬁnition, three rules of series circuits follow: all c omponents share the same current;
resistances add to equal a larger , total resistance; and vol tage drops add to equal a larger , total
voltage. All of these rules ﬁnd root in the deﬁnition of a seri es circuit. If you understand that
deﬁnition fully , then the rules are nothing more than footno tes to the deﬁnition.
