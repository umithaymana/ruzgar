with increasing temperature. This is known as derating. Many power device datasheets in-

4.16 BJT quirks
An ideal transistor would show 0% distortion in amplifying a signal. Its gain would extend
to all frequencies. It would control hundreds of amperes of c urrent, at hundreds of degrees C.
In practice, available devices show distortion. Ampliﬁcat ion is limited at the high frequency
end of the spectrum. Real parts only handle tens of amperes wi th precautions. Care must be
taken when paralleling transistors for higher current. Ope ration at elevated temperatures can
destroy transistors if precautions are not taken.
