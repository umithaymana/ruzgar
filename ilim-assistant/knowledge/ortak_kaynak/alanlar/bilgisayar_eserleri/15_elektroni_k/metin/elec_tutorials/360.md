lator circuits. An oscillator is a device that produces an alternating (AC), or at least pul sing,
output voltage. Technically , it is known as an astable device: having no stable output state (no
equilibrium whatsoever). Oscillators are very useful devi ces, and they are easily made with
just an op-amp and a few external components.
