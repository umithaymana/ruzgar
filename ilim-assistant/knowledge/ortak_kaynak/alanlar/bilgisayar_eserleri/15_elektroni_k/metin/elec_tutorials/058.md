Once the victim has been safely disconnected from the source of electric power , the im-
mediate medical concerns for the victim should be respirati on and circulation (breathing and

If the victim is conscious, it is best to have them lie still un til qualiﬁed emergency response

larities or a heart attack up to several hours later , so the vi ctim should pay close attention to
their own condition after the incident, ideally under super vision.
