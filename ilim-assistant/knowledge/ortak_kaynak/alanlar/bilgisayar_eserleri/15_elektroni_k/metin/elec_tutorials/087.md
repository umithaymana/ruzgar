• REVIEW:
• To analyze a series-parallel combination circuit, follow these steps:
• Reduce the original circuit to a single equivalent resisto r , re-drawing the circuit in each
step of reduction as simple series and simple parallel parts are reduced to single, equiv-
alent resistors.
• Solve for total resistance.
• Solve for total current (I=E/R).
• Determine equivalent resistor voltage drops and branch cu rrents one stage at a time,
working backwards to the original circuit conﬁguration aga in.
