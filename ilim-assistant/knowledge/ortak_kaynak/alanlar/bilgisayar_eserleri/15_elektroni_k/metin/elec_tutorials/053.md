Suppose I were to place my two hands across the terminals of an AC voltage source at

necessary in this clean, dry state of skin condition to produ ce a current of 20 milliamps (enough

E = IR
E = (20 mA)(1 M Ω)
E = 20,000 volts, or 20 kV
Bear in mind that this is a ”best case” scenario (clean, dry sk in) from the standpoint of
