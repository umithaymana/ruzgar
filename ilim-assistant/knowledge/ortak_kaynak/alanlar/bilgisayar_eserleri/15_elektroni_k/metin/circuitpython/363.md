8.13.3 Bias current
Inputs on an op-amp have extremely high input impedances. Th at is, the input currents enter-
ing or exiting an op-amp’s two input signal connections are e xtremely small. For most purposes

cuit as though there was absolutely zero current entering or exiting the input connections.
