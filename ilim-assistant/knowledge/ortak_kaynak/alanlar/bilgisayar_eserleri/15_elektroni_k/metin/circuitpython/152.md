1.1 From electric to electronic
This third volume of the book series Lessons In Electric Circuits makes a departure from the
former two in that the transition between electric circuits and electronic circuits is formally
crossed. Electric circuits are connections of conductive w ires and other devices whereby the
uniform ﬂow of electrons occurs. Electronic circuits add a n ew dimension to electric circuits
in that some means of control is exerted over the ﬂow of electrons by another electrical si gnal,
either a voltage or a current.
