The positive feedback mentioned earlier in this chapter is c learly evident here. When one
transistor breaks down, it allows current through the devic e structure. This current may be
viewed as the ”output” signal of the device. Once an output cu rrent is established, it works
to hold both transistors in saturation, thus ensuring the co ntinuation of a substantial output

current) to keep both transistors in the ”on” state, thus rei nforcing (or regenerating) itself.
