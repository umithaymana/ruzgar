In both output formats, the left-hand column of numbers repr esents the battery voltage at
each interval, as it increases from 0 volts to 100 volts, 5 vol ts at a time. The numbers in the
right-hand column indicate the circuit current for each of t hose voltages. Look closely at those
numbers and you’ll see the proportional relationship betwe en each pair: Ohm’s Law (I=E/R)
holds true in each and every case, each current value being 1/ 5 the respective voltage value,
because the circuit resistance is exactly 5 Ω. Again, the negative numbers for current in this
SPICE analysis is more of a quirk than anything else. Just pay attention to the absolute value
of each number unless otherwise speciﬁed.
