Notice the voltage drops across each resistor , and how the su m of the voltage drops (1.5 +
5 + 2.5) is equal to the battery (supply) voltage: 9 volts. Thi s is the third principle of series
circuits: that the supply voltage is equal to the sum of the in dividual voltage drops.
