at any point. If we were to monitor a cross-section of the wire in a single circuit, counting the
electrons ﬂowing by , we would notice the exact same quantity per unit of time as in any other

• REVIEW:
• Electrons can be motivated to ﬂow through a conductor by the same force manifested in
static electricity .
• Voltage is the measure of speciﬁc potential energy (potential energ y per unit charge) be-
tween two locations. In layman’s terms, it is the measure of ” push” available to motivate
electrons.
• Voltage, as an expression of potential energy , is always re lative between two locations, or
points. Sometimes it is called a voltage ”drop.”
