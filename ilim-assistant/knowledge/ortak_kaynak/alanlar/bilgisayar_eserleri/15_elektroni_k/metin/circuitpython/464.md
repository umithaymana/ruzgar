Notice that in this parallel circuit, an open branch only aff ects the current through that
branch and the circuit’s total current. Total voltage – bein g shared equally across all compo-
nents in a parallel circuit, will be the same for all resistor s. Due to the fact that the voltage
source’s tendency is to hold voltage constant, its voltage will not change, and being in parallel
with all the resistors, it will hold all the resistors’ volta ges the same as they were before: 9
volts. Being that voltage is the only common parameter in a pa rallel circuit, and the other re-
sistors haven’t changed resistance value, their respectiv e branch currents remain unchanged.
