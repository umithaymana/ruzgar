This principle is known as Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law (discovered in 1847 by Gustav R. Kirch-
hoff, a German physicist), and it can be stated as such:
”The algebraic sum of all voltages in a loop must equal zero”
By algebraic, I mean accounting for signs (polarities) as well as magnitu des. By loop, I
mean any path traced from one point in a circuit around to othe r points in that circuit, and
ﬁnally back to the initial point. In the above example the loo p was formed by following points
in this order: 1-2-3-4-1. It doesn’t matter which point we st art at or which direction we proceed
in tracing the loop; the voltage sum will still equal zero. To demonstrate, we can tally up the
voltages in loop 3-2-1-4-3 of the same circuit:
