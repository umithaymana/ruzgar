As daunting a task as series-parallel AC circuit analysis ma y appear , it must be emphasized
that there is nothing really new going on here besides the use of complex numbers. Ohm’s Law
(in its new form of E=IZ) still holds true, as do the voltage an d current Laws of Kirchhoff.
While there is more potential for human error in carrying out the necessary complex number
calculations, the basic principles and techniques of serie s-parallel circuit reduction are exactly
the same.
