Integrating Even and Odd Functions
We saw inFunctions and Graphsthat an even function is a function in whichf (−x) = f (x) for all x in the
domain—that is, the graph of the curve is unchanged whenx is replaced with −x. The graphs of even functions are
symmetricaboutthe y-axis.Anoddfunctionisoneinwhich f (−x) = −f (x) for allxinthedomain,andthegraphofthe
function is symmetric about the origin.
Integrals of even functions, when the limits of integration are from −a to a, involve two equal areas, because they are
symmetric aboutthe y-axis.Integrals ofoddfunctions,whenthelimits ofintegration aresimilarly [−a, a], evaluate to
zero because the areas above and below the x-axis are equal.
