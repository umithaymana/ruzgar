Simplify Fractions A fraction is considered simplified if there are no common factors, other than 1, in its numerator and denominator. For example, 2 3 2 3 is simplified because there are no common factors of 2 and 3. 10 15 10 15 is not simplified because 5 5 is a common factor of 10 and 15. Simplified Fraction A fraction is considered simplified if there are no common factors in its numerator and denominator. The phrase reduce a fraction means to simplify the fraction. We simplify, or reduce, a fraction by removing the common factors of the numerator and denominator. A fraction is not simplified until all common factors have been removed. If an expression has fractions, it is not completely simplified until the fractions are simplified. In Example 1.64 , we used the equivalent fractions property to find equivalent fractions.
