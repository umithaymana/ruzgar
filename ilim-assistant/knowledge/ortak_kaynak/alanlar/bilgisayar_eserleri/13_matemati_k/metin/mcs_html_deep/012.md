Division is the inverse operation of multiplication. So, 15 ÷ 3 = 5 15 ÷ 3 = 5 because 5 · 3 = 15 . 5 · 3 = 15 . In words, this expression says that 15 can be divided into three groups of five each because adding five three times gives 15.
