(b) How much work is required to stretch it an additional 6 cm? Strategy Work “required” means work done against the spring force, which is the negative of the work in Equation 7.5 , that is W = 1 2 k ( x B 2 − x A 2 ) . W = 1 2 k ( x B 2 − x A 2 ) .
