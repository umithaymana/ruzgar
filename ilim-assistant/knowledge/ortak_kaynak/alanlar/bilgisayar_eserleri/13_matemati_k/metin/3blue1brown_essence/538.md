Figure 5.61 Balloons lift off at the 2001 2001 Albuquerque International Balloon Fiesta. (credit: David Herrera, Flickr) As the name implies, hot air balloons use hot air to generate lift. Once the balloon takes off, the pilot controls the altitude of the balloon, either by using the burner to heat the air and ascend or by using a vent near the top of the balloon to release heated air and descend. The pilot has very little control over where the balloon goes, however—balloons are at the mercy of the winds.
