Play the Circuit Game The Math Behind FHE These lecture notes explain how the CKKS scheme uses ring theory to construct fully homomorphic encryption. The security of CKKS relies on the ring learning with errors (RLWE) assumption: roughly, that adding a small amount of noise to an equation of ring elements makes it hard to solve. CKKS encrypts data by representing batches of complex numbers in terms of ring elements and adding a small amount of noise, making it possible to do approximate arithmetic on encrypted data in a single-instruction multiple-data format.
