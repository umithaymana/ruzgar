This chapter constitutes the core of any first course on linear algebra: eigenvalues and eigenvectors play a crucial role in most real-world applications of the subject. In Section 5.1 , we will define eigenvalues and eigenvectors, and show how to compute the latter; in Section 5.2 we will learn to compute the former.
