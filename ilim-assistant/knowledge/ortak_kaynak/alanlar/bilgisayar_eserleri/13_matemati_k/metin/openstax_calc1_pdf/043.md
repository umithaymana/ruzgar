It is important to understand that when we write statements such aslimx → af (x) = +∞ or limx → af (x) = −∞ we are

limit of a functionf (x) to exist ata, it must approach a real numberL as x approaches a. That said, if, for example,
limx → af (x) = +∞, we always write limx → af (x) = +∞ rather than limx → af (x) DNE.
