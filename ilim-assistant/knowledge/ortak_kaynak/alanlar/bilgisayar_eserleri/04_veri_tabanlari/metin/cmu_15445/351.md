Each field has three parts: name, type and value. Values may be text (String, Reader or pre-analyzed TokenStream), binary (byte[]), or numeric (a Number). Fields are optionally stored in the index, so that they may be returned with hits on the document. NOTE: the field type is an IndexableFieldType . Making changes to the state of the IndexableFieldType will impact any Field it is used in. It is strongly recommended that no changes be made after Field instantiation.
