Consider an time-varying affine (approximation of a) continuous-time dynamical system in state-space form: $$\dot{\bx} = {\bf A}(t)\bx + {\bf B}(t)\bu + {\bf c}(t),$$ and a running cost function in the general quadratic form: \begin{gather*} \ell(t, \bx,\bu) = \begin{bmatrix} \bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}^T {\bf Q}(t) \begin{bmatrix} \bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} \bu \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}^T {\bf R}(t) \begin{bmatrix} \bu \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} + 2\bx^T{\bf N}(t)\bu, \\ \forall t\in[t_0, t_f], \quad \bQ = \begin{bmatrix} \bQ_{xx} & \bq_x \\ \bq_x^T & q_0 \end{bmatrix}, \bQ_{xx} \succeq 0, \quad \bR(t) = \begin{bmatrix} \bR_{uu} & {\bf r}_u \\ {\bf r}_u^T & r_0 \end{bmatrix}, \bR_{uu} \succ 0.\end{gather*} It's tempting to require that the cost is always positive, e.g. with conditions of the form $r_0 - \bf{r}_u^T \bR_{uu}^{-1} {\bf r}_u \ge 0$, but this is not actually necessary in the finite-horizon case. Observe that our LQR "optimal tracking" derivation fits in this form, as we can always write $$(\bx - \bx_d(t))^T \bQ_t (\bx - \bx_d(t)) + (\bu - \bu_d(t))^T \bR_t (\bu - \bu_d(t)) + 2 (\bx - \bx_d(t))^T {\bf N}_t (\bu - \bu_d(t)),$$ by taking \begin{gather*} \bQ_{xx} = \bQ_t,\quad \bq_x = -\bQ_t \bx_d - {\bf N}_t\bu_d, \quad q_0 = \bx_d^T \bQ_t \bx_d + 2 \bx_d^T {\bf N}_t \bu_d, \\ \bR_{uu} = \bR_t, \quad {\bf r}_u = -\bR_t \bu_d - {\bf N}_t^T \bx_d, \quad r_0 = \bu_d^T \bR_t \bu_d, \quad {\bf N} = {\bf N}_t.\end{gather*} Of course, we can also add a quadratic final cost with $\bQ_f$. Let's search for a positive quadratic, time-varying cost-to-go function of the form: \begin{gather*} J(t, \bx)=\begin{bmatrix} \bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}^T {\bf S}(t) \begin{bmatrix} \bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad {\bf S}(t) = \begin{bmatrix} {\bf S}_{xx} & {\bf s}_x \\ {\bf s}_x^T & s_0 \end{bmatrix}, {\bf S}_{xx} \succeq 0, \\ \frac{\partial J}{\partial \bx} = 2\bx^T{\bf S}_{xx} + 2{\bf s}_x^T, \quad \frac{\partial J}{\partial t} = \begin{bmatrix} \bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}^T\dot{\bf S} \begin{bmatrix} \bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}. \end{gather*} Writing out the HJB: \begin{gather*} \min_\bu \left[\ell(\bx,\bu) + \frac{\partial J}{\partial \bx} \left[{\bf A}(t)\bx + {\bf B}(t)\bu + {\bf c}(t) \right] + \frac{\partial J}{\partial t} \right] = 0, \end{gather*} we can find the minimizing $\bu$ with \begin{gather*} \frac{\partial}{\partial \bu} = 2\bu^T{\bf R}_{uu} + 2{\bf r}_u^T + 2\bx^T{\bf N} + (2\bx^T{\bf S}_{xx} + 2{\bf s}_x^T){\bf B} = 0 \\ \bu^* = -{\bf R}_{uu}^{-1} \begin{bmatrix} {\bf N} + {\bf S}_{xx} \bB \\ {\bf r}^T_{u} + {\bf s}^T_{x}\bB \end{bmatrix}^T \begin{bmatrix} \bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = -{\bf K}(t) \begin{bmatrix} \bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = -{\bf K}_x(t) \bx - {\bf k}_0(t). \end{gather*} Inserting this back into the HJB gives us the updated Riccati differential equation. Since this must hold for all $\bx$, we can collect the quadratic, linear, and offset terms and set them each individually equal to zero, yielding: \begin{align*} -\dot{\bf S}_{xx} =& \bQ_{xx} - ({\bf N} + {\bf S}_{xx} \bB){\bf R}_{uu}^{-1}({\bf N} + {\bf S}_{xx} \bB)^T + {\bf S}_{xx}{\bf A} + {\bf A}^T{\bf S}_{xx}, \\ -\dot{\bf s}_x =& \bq_x - ({\bf N} + {\bf S}_{xx} \bB){\bf R}_{uu}^{-1} ({\bf r}_u + \bB^T {\bf s}_x ) + {\bf A}^T{\bf s}_x + {\bf S}_{xx}{\bf c}, \\ -\dot{s}_0 =& q_0 + r_0 - ({\bf r}_u + \bB^T {\bf s}_x)^T{\bf R}_{uu}^{-1} ({\bf r}_u + \bB^T {\bf s}_x) + 2{\bf s}_x^T {\bf c}, \end{align*} with the final conditions ${\bf S}(t_f) = {\bf Q}_f.$ Provide the sqrt formulation of the S1 equation here In the discrete-time version, we have $$\bx[n+1] = {\bf A}[n] \bx[n] + {\bf B}[n] \bu[n] + {\bf c}[n],$$ and a running cost function of the form \begin{gather*} \ell(n, \bx,\bu) = \begin{bmatrix} \bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}^T {\bf Q}[n] \begin{bmatrix} \bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} \bu \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}^T {\bf R}[n] \begin{bmatrix} \bu \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} + 2\bx^T{\bf N}[n]\bu, \\ \forall n \in [n_0, n_f] \in \mathbb{Z}, \quad \bQ[n] = \begin{bmatrix} \bQ_{xx} & \bq_x \\ \bq_x^T & q_0 \end{bmatrix}, \bQ_{xx} \succeq 0, \quad \bR[n] = \begin{bmatrix} \bR_{uu} & {\bf r}_u \\ {\bf r}_u^T & r_0 \end{bmatrix}, \bR_{uu} \succ 0. \end{gather*} and a terminal cost evaluated at $\bx[n_f]$ of the form $$\ell_f(\bx) = \begin{bmatrix} \bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}^T {\bf Q}_f \begin{bmatrix} \bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}.$$ Let's search for a positive quadratic, time-varying cost-to-go of the form: \begin{gather*} J(n,\bx) = \begin{bmatrix} \bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} {\bf S}[n]\begin{bmatrix}\bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}, \,{\bf S}[n] = \begin{bmatrix} {\bf S}_{xx} & {\bf s}_x \\ {\bf s}_x^T & s_0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad {\bf S}_{xx} \succeq 0, s_0 - \bf{s}_x^T {\bf S}_{xx}^+ \bf{s}_x \ge 0. \end{gather*} Writing out the HJB: $$J(n, \bx) = \min_\bu \left[\ell(n, \bx,\bu) + J(n+1, {\bf A}[n]\bx + {\bf B}[n]\bu + {\bf c}[n]) \right],$$ we can find the minimizing $\bu$ (with the time dependence suppressed for brevity) with \begin{gather*} \frac{\partial}{\partial \bu} = 2\bu^T{\bf R}_{uu} + 2{\bf r}_u^T + 2\bx^T{\bf N} + 2((\bA\bx + \bB\bu + {\bf c})^T{\bf S}_{xx} + {\bf s}_x^T){\bf B} = 0 \\ \bu^* = -({\bf R}_{uu} + \bB^T{\bf S}_{xx}\bB)^{-1} \begin{bmatrix} {\bf N} + \bA^T{\bf S}_{xx} \bB, \\ {\bf r}^T_{u} + {\bf c}^T{\bf S}_{xx} \bB + {\bf s}^T_{x}\bB \end{bmatrix}^T \begin{bmatrix} \bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = -{\bf K}[n] \begin{bmatrix} \bx \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = -{\bf K}_x[n] \bx - {\bf k}_0[n]. \end{gather*} Inserting this back into the HJB gives us the updated Riccati difference equation, we obtain: \begin{align*} {\bf S}_{xx}[n] =& \, \bQ_{xx} + \bA^T{\bf S}_{xx}\bA - ({\bf N}+\bA^T{\bf S}_{xx}\bB)(\bR_{uu}+\bB^T{\bf S}_{xx}\bB)^{-1}({\bf N}+\bA^T{\bf S}_{xx}\bB)^T, \\ {\bf s}_x[n] =& \,\bq_x + \bA^T{\bf S}_{xx} c + \bA^T{\bf s}_x \\ &- ({\bf N}+\bA^T{\bf S}_{xx}\bB)(\bR_{uu}+\bB^T{\bf S}_{xx}\bB)^{-1}({\bf r}_u + \bB^T{\bf S}_{xx} {\bf c} + \bB^T{\bf s}_x), \\ s_0[n] =&\, q_0 + r_0 + s_0 + {\bf c}^T{\bf S}_{xx}{\bf c} + 2 {\bf c}^T {\bf s}_x \\ &- ({\bf r}_u + \bB^T{\bf S}_{xx} {\bf c} + \bB^T{\bf s}_x)^T(\bR_{uu}+\bB^T{\bf S}_{xx}\bB)^{-1}({\bf r}_u + \bB^T{\bf S}_{xx} {\bf c} + \bB^T{\bf s}_x). \end{align*} where the ${\bf S}$ terms on the right hand side evaluated at time $n+1$, and all remaining time-dependent terms ($\bA, \bB,$ etc) are evaluated at time $n$.
