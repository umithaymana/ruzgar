In inverse time feed rate mode, an F word means the move should be completed in (one divided by the F number) minutes. For example, F2.0 means the move should be completed in a half a minute. When the inverse time feed rate mode is active, an F word must appear on every line which has a G1 , G2 , or G3 motion, and an F word on a line that does not have G1 , G2 , or G3 is ignored. Being in inverse time feed rate mode does not affect G0 (rapid move) motions.
