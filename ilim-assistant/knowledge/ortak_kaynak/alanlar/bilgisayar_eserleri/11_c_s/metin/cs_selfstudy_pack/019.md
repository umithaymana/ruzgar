The assignments and exams for each course are to prepare you to use your knowledge to solve real-world problems. After you've completed Core CS and the parts of Advanced CS relevant to you, you should identify a problem that you can solve using the knowledge you've acquired. You can create something entirely new, or you can improve some tool/program that you use and wish were better. Students who would like more guidance in creating a project may choose to use a series of project oriented courses.
