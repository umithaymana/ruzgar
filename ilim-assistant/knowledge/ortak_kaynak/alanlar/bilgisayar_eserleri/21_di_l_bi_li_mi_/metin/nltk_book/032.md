Let's inspect some tagged text to see what parts of speech occur before a noun, with the most frequent ones first. To begin with, we construct a list of bigrams whose members are themselves word-tag pairs such as (( 'The' , 'DET' ), ( 'Fulton' , 'NP' )) and (( 'Fulton' , 'NP' ), ( 'County' , 'N' )) . Then we construct a FreqDist from the tag parts of the bigrams. &nbsp; &gt;&gt;&gt; word_tag_pairs = nltk.bigrams(brown_news_tagged) &gt;&gt;&gt; noun_preceders = [a[1] for (a, b) in word_tag_pairs if b[1] == 'NOUN' ] &gt;&gt;&gt; fdist = nltk.FreqDist(noun_preceders) &gt;&gt;&gt; [tag for (tag, _) in fdist.most_common()] ['NOUN', 'DET', 'ADJ', 'ADP', '.', 'VERB', 'CONJ', 'NUM', 'ADV', 'PRT', 'PRON', 'X'] This confirms our assertion that nouns occur after determiners and adjectives, including numeral adjectives (tagged as NUM ). 2.5&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Verbs Verbs are words that describe events and actions, e.g. fall , eat in 2.3 .
