UD_Abaza-ATB is a treebank based on Spoken corpus of Abaza . This corpus represents a compilation of spoken texts collected during field-trips to Inžič-Čukun village in Karachay-Cherkess Republic, Russia. Abaza is a highly polysynthetic language from the Northwest Caucasian family, and it is spoken by approximately 38.000 speakers in Russia.
