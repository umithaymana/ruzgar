Piper Voice Samples Below are samples for Piper , a fast and local text to speech system. Samples were generated from the first paragraph of the Wikipedia entry for rainbow . Language Voice Quality Speaker Download Quality Voices are trained at one of 4 "quality" levels: x_low - 16Khz audio, 5-7M params low - 16Khz audio, 15-20M params medium - 22.05Khz audio, 15-20M params high - 22.05Khz audio, 28-32M params Multi-Speaker Some voices contain multiple speakers, which captures the style of multiple people within a single model. Multi-speaker models can quickly switch between different speakers, but the quality of an individual speaker may be less than a single speaker model.
