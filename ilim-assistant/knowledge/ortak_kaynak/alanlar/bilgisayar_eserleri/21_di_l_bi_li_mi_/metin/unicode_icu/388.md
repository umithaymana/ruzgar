UnicodeSet syntax was first developed in 1999 as part of the ICU project, with much of the work being carried out by Alan Liu; the syntax was enriched over the years, and extensions were implemented in the Unicode tools by Mark Davis. This document builds upon an earlier specification of UnicodeSet syntax using a scannerless grammar written in 2018 by Mark Davis, which was included in Unicode Technical Standard #35, Locale Data Markup Language. That specification had benefited from careful review and corrections by Niels Saurer in 2023, which laid the groundwork for the restructured specification in this document. This specification was developed in coordination with the ICU Technical Committee, in order to ensure the adequacy of the standard for its main implementer, and in order to evaluate questions of backward compatibility. Thanks in particular to Markus Scherer and Rich Gillam for their feedback during ICU-TC discussions, which resulted in simplifications of the syntax. Roozbeh Pournader extensively reviewed the document for correctness and identified many important edge cases.
