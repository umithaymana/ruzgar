Property kJinmeiyoKanji Status Provisional Category Other Mappings Introduced 11.0 Delimiter N/A Syntax (20\d{2})(:U\+[23]?[0-9A-F]{4})? Description The year that corresponds to the Jinmei-yō Kanji (人名用漢字) table in which the ideograph appears, and followed by a colon and the code point of its standard form if it is considered a variant. Published by Japan’s Ministry of Justice (法務省) in 2010, and amended in 2015, 2017, and 2026 with one additional ideograph during each year, the Jinmei-yō Kanji table (人名用漢字表) includes 864 ideographs for use in personal names in Japan. The version year is either 2010 (861 ideographs), 2015 (one ideograph), 2017 (one ideograph), or 2026 (one ideograph), and 230 ideographs are variants for which the code point of the standard Japanese form is specified.
